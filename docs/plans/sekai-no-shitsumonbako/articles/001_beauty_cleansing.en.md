# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about cleansing?｜Japan Questions #1

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/cleansing` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

How much cleansing you need, and how strong it should be, is argued about in communities everywhere. Japan adds a habit of its own: "double cleansing" (W洗顔, daburu-senga — an oil-based cleanser followed by a foaming face wash). Drugstore shelves (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.) are full of products marked "W洗顔不要" (no second wash needed), which adds to the confusion.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "the more thoroughly it removes, the better." What you are removing and how strong the cleanser is are two separate questions.

A cleanser (クレンジング, kurenjingu) removes mainly **oil-based dirt**: make-up, sunscreen and sebum. It uses oils or surfactants to loosen what water alone cannot shift.

## Background｜What a cosmetic may claim is fixed by law at 56 items

In Japan, the effects a cosmetic may state on its label or in advertising are **limited to 56 items by a notice from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) (Yakushoku-hatsu 0721 No. 1, 21 July 2011)**. "Cleans the skin by washing" and "conditions the skin" are within that range. **Wording such as "makes skin problems go away" or "improves" is outside it.**

In other words, the most that can be said about a cleanser is that it **removes**. Any description that promises more is inaccurate, both legally and factually.

## Misunderstanding 1｜You need the same cleanser on days you wear no make-up

Cleansers are designed for oil-based dirt. **On a day when you have used neither sunscreen nor make-up, there is no need for a product of the same strength.** It is sensible to change how you cleanse according to what you put on that day.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The stronger the cleansing power, the better

Removing more oil than necessary puts a strain on the skin surface's ability to hold water. **If your skin feels tight after washing, take it as a sign the product was too strong for your skin that day.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜The longer you take, the cleaner you get

The longer you keep rubbing, the greater the load from friction. **The basic rule is to follow the time and amount stated on the product.** A product that works quickly, used briefly, is gentler than rubbing long and hard.

## Misunderstanding 4｜"No second wash needed" means you must never use a face wash

That label describes how the product is designed. **Depending on the type of sunscreen you used and the state of your skin, a face wash may still suit you better.** Read the label, then judge by your own skin.

## Misunderstanding 5｜You can see whether everything has come off

Leftover sunscreen or sebum is not always visible. **The hairline, the chin and the sides of the nose are known as places where residue tends to remain.**

## How VV sees it

VV does not want skincare to become a contest of strength. **Change how you cleanse according to what you used that day.** That alone reduces the load on your skin.
