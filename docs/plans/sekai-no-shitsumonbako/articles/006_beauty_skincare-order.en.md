# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about the order of skincare steps?｜Japan Questions #6

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/skincare-order` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

The order in which to apply skincare is debated worldwide, and recommendations differ by country and by brand. Japanese routines also use products a newcomer may not recognise, such as lotion (化粧水, keshōsui — a watery, toner-like liquid) and milky lotion (乳液, nyūeki — a light emulsion). And Japan has a **legal line** that frames the whole debate.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "there is one correct order that applies everywhere." There is a rough guide, but the product's own directions come first.

The basic pattern usually given is: **in the morning, "face wash → lotion → milky lotion → sunscreen"; at night, "cleanser → face wash → lotion → milky lotion."**

## Background｜Cosmetics may claim no more than 56 things

Under a notice from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) (Yakushoku-hatsu 0721 No. 1, 21 July 2011), the effects a cosmetic may state on its label or in advertising are **limited to 56 items**. "Conditions the skin" and "moisturises the skin" are within the range. **Claims such as "the wrong order damages your skin" or "the right order doubles the effect" fall outside it.**

So the debate about order can honestly cover only **ease of use and lightness of load on the skin**.

## Misunderstanding 1｜One order works for every product

**From the most watery to the most oily** is a widely shared rule of thumb. But some products are designed not to fit it, and **the product's directions are the final standard.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Get the order wrong and everything is wasted

There is no evidence that a different order, on its own, damages the skin. **Worrying about it and adding steps can be a greater load in terms of friction.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜The more layers, the better

Each extra step is another touch on the skin. **Keeping to a number of steps you can sustain is more realistic.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Morning and night can be the same

Morning is about preparing for daytime UV; night is about removing. **The purposes differ, so there is no reason they must be identical.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜The same routine all year round

Humidity and temperature change the state of your skin. **You do not need to keep the same routine through every season.**

## How VV sees it

VV believes that **whether you can keep it up** matters more than the order. Start by using products as directed, then pare the routine down to what suits you. We think that is enough.
