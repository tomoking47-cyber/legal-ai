# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about pillow height?｜Japan Questions #7

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/pillow-height` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Low pillow or high pillow? Opinions divide around the world, and if you have just moved to Japan you may find the pillows on sale thinner or firmer than you are used to. Look at the numbers, and the reason people disagree becomes clear.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "there is a height that suits everyone." The right height depends on build and sleeping position, and the guide ranges differ by several centimetres.

The guide figures given by bedding makers fall roughly within these ranges.

| Sleeping position | Guide height | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| On your back | **about 1–6 cm** | Chin slightly tucked; gaze falls a little towards your feet rather than straight at the ceiling |
| On your side | **about 4–10 cm** | Ear, shoulder and hip in a straight line |
| Face down | about 0.5–3 cm | Lower than the other positions |

**The same person needs nearly twice the height on their side as on their back.** That is why no "height for everyone" exists.

> **[Verify]** The figures above are guide values from bedding makers' own guidance, not standards set by a public body. **Before publication, confirm the source (each maker's official material) for every figure printed.**

## Why height matters

The bones of the neck (cervical spine) curve gently forwards and carry the weight of the head. **Too low a pillow lets the chin rise and the neck arch back; too high a pillow tucks the chin too far and strains the back of the neck.** Either way, the muscles at the back of the neck tend to stay locked in tension.

## Misunderstanding 1｜The lower, the healthier

Too low, and the pillow may fail to support the neck's natural curve. **It is not as simple as "lower is better."**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Height is all that matters

How far your head sinks in changes the real height, even when the label gives the same number. **Look at material and firmness together with height.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜You can choose a pillow separately from the mattress

On a soft mattress the shoulder sinks, and the pillow height you need drops. **The pillow is decided in combination with the mattress.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Once it fits, it fits for ever

Pillows flatten with use, and your build and sleeping habits change. **Plan to review it regularly.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Morning aches are always the pillow's fault

Neck and shoulder trouble has many possible causes. **If it persists, do not try to solve it by changing pillows; see a doctor.**
