# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about using the fridge?｜Japan Questions #8

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/using-the-fridge` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

How people use a fridge is where household habits show most. But the temperature inside is set by numbers, not habit. Japanese fridges usually have more separate compartments than many readers from abroad are used to, so it helps to know what each one is for.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "everything cools the same wherever you put it." Temperatures inside can differ by 20°C or more, and where you put food changes how long it keeps.

## Temperature zones inside (as described by makers)

| Compartment | Guide temperature | Suited to |
|---|---|---|
| Main fridge compartment (冷蔵室) | **about 0–6°C** | Cooked dishes, drinks, condiments |
| Chilled compartment (チルド室, chirudo-shitsu) | **about 0–2°C** | Meat, fish, processed meat (just above freezing) |
| Vegetable compartment (野菜室, yasai-shitsu) | **about 4–8°C** | Vegetables and fruit (not too cold; keeps humidity) |
| Freezer (冷凍室) | **about −18 to −20°C** | Frozen food, ingredients you freeze yourself |

The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) pamphlet "Six points for preventing food poisoning at home" gives **10°C or below for the fridge and −15°C or below for the freezer** as a guide. Makers' standard settings are lower than this, leaving a margin.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It is the same temperature everywhere inside

Door pockets warm up each time the door opens; the back is more stable. **The basic rule is not to put perishable food in the door.** Some fridges are designed with an egg tray in the door, but that does not change the fact that it is a spot exposed to temperature swings.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The fuller, the more efficient

Cold air circulates to cool the contents. Overfill, and the air paths are blocked, **leaving spots that do not cool well.** The "six points" also say to "take care not to overfill" the fridge.

## Misunderstanding 3｜The freezer should also be kept from getting full

The freezer is the opposite. **Frozen items act as ice packs for each other, so makers advise that a reasonably full freezer holds its temperature more steadily.** Fridge and freezer follow different logic.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Refrigeration is right for every ingredient

Potatoes and some vegetables and fruit can change in quality at low temperatures (chilling injury). **Some are better stored at room temperature.** This is why the vegetable compartment is set warmer than the main compartment.

## Misunderstanding 5｜The same setting all year round

In summer, both the outside temperature and the number of door openings rise. **Review the setting with the season.**

## Misunderstanding 6｜A power cut of a few hours is fine

The temperature inside rises faster than you might expect. **Keep the door shut, and check the state of the food once power returns.**
