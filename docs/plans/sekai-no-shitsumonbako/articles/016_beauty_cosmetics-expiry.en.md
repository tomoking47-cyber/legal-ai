# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about cosmetic expiry dates?｜Japan Questions #16

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/cosmetics-expiry` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Many cosmetics sold in Japan carry no expiry date, and visitors and residents from abroad often ask why. It is not an oversight. It is how the rules are designed.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "no date printed means it lasts for ever." No date is printed because the product is designed to "keep its quality unopened for 3 years or more," and there is a separate guide for after opening.

## How the rules work

Japan's Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act (the country's cosmetics and drugs law) requires an expiry date only on **cosmetics that could deteriorate within 3 years of manufacture.** A 1980 (Shōwa 55) notice of the then Ministry of Health and Welfare names three types:

| Must show an expiry date | Reason |
|---|---|
| Products containing ascorbic acid (vitamin C)-type ingredients | Oxidise easily |
| Products containing enzymes | Activity changes |
| Products at risk of changing in character or quality within 3 years under proper storage | Comparable to the above |

All other cosmetics are exempt from date labelling on the assumption that they **keep their quality for 3 years unopened and properly stored.**

> **[Verify]** Several specialist websites present this as a summary based on Article 61 of the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act and the 1980 Ministry of Health and Welfare notice. **Confirm against the Japan Cosmetic Industry Association "Cosmetics Q&A" and the original legal texts.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜No date, so it can be used for ever

No label and no deterioration are two different things. **The lot number printed on the base or back of the container is a clue to when it was made.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Unopened and opened are the same

The 3-year assumption is for **unopened** products. Once opened, the product meets air and hands. **The industry's general guidance is to use products up within a few months to a year of opening.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜If it looks the same, it is fine

Separation, odour and colour change are obvious signs, but **change is not always visible.** Do not judge by appearance alone.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Refrigerating extends the life

Chilling does not extend the period after opening. **The basics of storage are to avoid direct sunlight, heat and humidity.** The washbasin area (洗面所, senmenjo) and bathroom are humid and unsuitable.

## Misunderstanding 5｜The tools you use do not matter

A dirty sponge or brush carries contamination into the product. **Think about the cleanliness of your tools as well.**
