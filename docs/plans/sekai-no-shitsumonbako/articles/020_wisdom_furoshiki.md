# Q. What do people get wrong about furoshiki in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #20

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/furoshiki` ／ Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

A furoshiki is a square cloth used to wrap and carry things. Abroad it is usually presented as a craft.

## A. The common misunderstanding is treating it as decoration. It is a tool whose shape is decided by what goes inside it.

One square of cloth becomes a different object depending on what it holds.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It is a traditional item no longer in use

It is sold in ordinary shops and used for gifts, lunch boxes, bottles and bags. **It did not stop being practical.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜There is one correct way to fold it

Different ties suit different shapes — flat, round, long, two bottles. **The fold follows the object, not a rulebook.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜The cloth is the point

The point is that one flat cloth replaces several rigid containers, then folds away to nothing. **It stores as a square and works as a bag.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Any fabric works the same

Size and weave change what it can carry and how the knot holds. **Choose the cloth for the load.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜It is only for gifts

It is also used as a bag, a cover, a cushion for fragile items and a wrap for luggage. **The gift use is the most visible one, not the only one.**
