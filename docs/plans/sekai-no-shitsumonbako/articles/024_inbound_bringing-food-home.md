# Q. What do people get wrong about bringing food home from Japan?｜Japan Questions #24

- Shelf: `inbound` ／ URL: `/en/questions/bringing-food-home` ／ **Caution: rules and regulations**
- Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

Food is one of the most popular things to carry home, and one of the most commonly confiscated.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that the question is about Japan. What matters is the rules of the country you are returning to.

Japan controls what comes in. **Your own country controls what you bring back.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜If it is sold in a shop, it can be taken home

Being on sale here says nothing about another country's import rules. **Check with the customs authority of your destination.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Sealed packaging makes it acceptable

Many countries restrict meat, dairy, fresh produce, seeds and plants **regardless of packaging.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Small amounts do not need declaring

Some countries require every food item to be declared, sealed or not. **Declaring costs nothing; failing to declare can carry a penalty.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Anything can go in hand luggage

Liquids, pastes and gels are restricted, and that includes some sauces and sweets. **Check your airline's rules.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Heat and time do not matter

Chocolate, fresh sweets and chilled items may not survive a long journey. **Look at the storage instructions before you buy.**
