# Q. What do people get wrong about single-purpose kitchen tools in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #30

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/single-purpose-kitchen-tools` ／ Status: **Draft — sources unverified**

Japanese kitchen shops are full of tools that do exactly one thing. From outside, this looks like clutter. The market data says it is a growth category.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that these are gimmicks. A narrow tool usually solves a task that happens often — and the shops that sell them are a ¥1.1 trillion market that grew by getting more specific.

## Two numbers

- **90.86 m²** — the average floor area of a Japanese dwelling (2023 Housing and Land Survey). Kitchens are small. Tools must be thin, flat, stackable and hangable.
- **¥1.11 trillion** — the fiscal-2025 size of the 100-yen shop market (Teikoku Databank), its third straight year above ¥1 trillion. Growth came from the **¥150–¥500 tier**: functional housework items, kitchen tools, DIY goods.

**Small kitchens create demand for specific tools. The retail sector grew by meeting it.**

> **[Verify]** Figures from the Statistics Bureau (2023 survey) and Teikoku Databank (April 2026). Confirm before publication.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Multi-purpose tools are always better

A tool that does five things often does none of them quickly. **If you do a task daily, a tool shaped for it saves time every day.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜They exist because kitchens are large

The opposite. **Small kitchens push tools to be slim and storable, not bulky and versatile.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜It is a modern retail trend

Specialised utensils have long existed in Japanese cooking. **The 100-yen shops made them cheap; they did not invent them.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜More tools means more washing

Many are designed to be rinsed in seconds. **Ease of cleaning is part of the design.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜They only work with Japanese cooking

A tool that drains, grates, peels or holds does the same job in any cuisine. **Buy for the task you already do.**
