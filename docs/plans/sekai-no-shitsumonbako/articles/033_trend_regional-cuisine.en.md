# Q. What do people get wrong about Japan's regional cuisine (kyōdo ryōri)?｜Japan Questions #33

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/regional-cuisine` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Regional dishes (郷土料理, kyōdo ryōri) are usually presented to visitors as local feasts. Japan's national database, which records 1,365 of them, tells a different story. Most began as ways of getting through an ordinary week with whatever the land offered.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is that regional dishes are "special-occasion feasts". Most are everyday cooking built around what the area could grow or catch, and the national government has recorded 1,365 dishes from all 47 prefectures.

## The national database "Uchi no Kyōdo Ryōri"

Since **fiscal 2019 (Reiwa 1)** the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) has been compiling a database, **"Uchi no Kyōdo Ryōri — precious flavours to pass on to the next generation" (うちの郷土料理〜次世代に伝えたい大切な味〜)**. It records around 30 dishes per prefecture, with their origins, history and recipes. **All 47 prefectures were covered by March 2022. The database holds 1,365 dishes.**

The aim is to hand each region's diverse food culture on to the next generation. It is published so that it can be used **for home cooking, restaurant menus, product development by food makers, and research**.

> **[Verify]** The number of dishes and the dates come from a MAFF press release (29 March 2022). **Check the original text before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜Most regional dishes are lavish

Where rice grew poorly, flour-based dishes developed; where food had to be kept, pickles and dried goods did. **Constraints created the dishes.** The database's notes also describe each dish's link to the local climate and produce.

## Misunderstanding 2｜There is one fixed recipe

Dishes with the same name are normally made differently from household to household and village to village. **Looking for the "correct" version does not match how they actually work.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜They have not changed since old times

Methods keep changing as ingredients and kitchen equipment change. **Changing is part of what a regional dish is.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜You can only eat them in that region

If you can get the ingredients, you can make them at home. **That is why the national database carries recipes.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜They are dishes for tourists

Most have survived as ordinary daily meals. **Being shown to tourists is a result, not the purpose.**
