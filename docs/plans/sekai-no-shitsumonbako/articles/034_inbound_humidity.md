Visitors check the forecast, see a familiar temperature, and pack accordingly. The number is only half the story.

## A. The common misunderstanding is reading temperature alone. At high humidity, sweat evaporates slowly, so the same number feels very different.

Thirty degrees in a dry climate and thirty degrees here are not the same experience.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It only matters in summer

The rainy season in early summer brings high humidity at moderate temperatures. **Laundry, shoes and bags all behave differently.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Thin clothing solves it

Fabric that moves air and dries quickly matters more than how little you wear. **Loose and breathable beats minimal.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Indoors is always comfortable

Air conditioning is often strong. **Carry a light layer for trains and shops.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Rain is the only reason to carry an umbrella

Umbrellas are also used against strong sun. **You will see plenty on clear days.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Humidity is only an inconvenience

High heat and humidity together raise the risk of heat-related illness, indoors as well as outdoors. **Drink regularly, use cooling, and seek medical help if you feel unwell.**
