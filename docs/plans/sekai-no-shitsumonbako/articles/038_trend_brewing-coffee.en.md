# Q. What do people get wrong about brewing coffee?｜Japan Questions #38

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/brewing-coffee` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Arguments about how to brew coffee never end, in Japan as everywhere else. What is less well known is which variable matters first. The guidance published by Japanese roasters puts freshness ahead of technique.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "brewing technique decides the taste". Beans start to deteriorate faster the moment they are ground: the guide is about two weeks for ground coffee and about one month for whole beans. Freshness takes effect first.

## Priorities in numbers

| Variable | Guide |
|---|---|
| Brewing temperature | **90–95°C** (194–203°F; about 95°C at the start of pouring, about 90°C at the end) |
| Storage as whole beans | **About one month** |
| Storage as ground coffee | **About two weeks** |

The speed of deterioration runs **ground coffee > roasted beans > green (unroasted) beans**. Grinding increases the surface area, so more of the coffee is exposed to oxygen and oxidation speeds up. The main cause of the loss of flavour is oxidation of the coffee oils that come to the surface of the bean during roasting.

> **[Verify]** The figures are guides based on information published by major Japanese coffee companies. **Check against the All Japan Coffee Association's materials or the makers' official materials before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜Boiling water is best

Water straight off the boil is generally described as bringing out bitterness and harsh notes. **90–95°C is the usual guide.** Some people also brew light roasts hotter to lift the aroma, and dark roasts cooler to hold back bitterness.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The finer the grind, the stronger and better the coffee

A finer grind extracts more, but **over-extraction can make astringency stand out.** Adjusting strength with the amount of coffee is easier to control.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Beans keep longer in the freezer

**Freezing is an option** if you cannot finish the beans within the period above. The conditions are an airtight container that keeps out oxygen, and avoiding the condensation caused by taking them in and out. **First of all, seal them and keep them away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Pre-ground is more convenient and just the same

As above, **grinding shortens the storage guide from one month to two weeks.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Expensive equipment makes better coffee

Equipment improves repeatability, but **getting the beans and the water right comes first.**
