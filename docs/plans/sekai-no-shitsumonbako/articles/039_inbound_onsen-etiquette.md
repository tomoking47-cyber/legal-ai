# Q. What do people get wrong about onsen etiquette in Japan?｜Japan Questions #39

- Shelf: `inbound` ／ URL: `/en/questions/onsen-etiquette` ／ Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

Most visitors know to wash first. The rules that trip people up are the smaller ones.

## A. The common misunderstanding is thinking of it as a bath. It is shared water that everyone enters already clean.

Washing is not preparation for the bath. **It is the condition for entering it.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜A quick rinse is enough

Wash and rinse thoroughly at the seated shower before entering. **No soap should reach the shared water.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜The small towel goes in the water

Keep it out of the bath — folded on your head or placed at the edge. **It is for drying, not for the water.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Swimwear is acceptable

Most facilities do not allow it in the bathing area. **Check the specific facility's rules before you go.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜You can dry off in the changing room

Wipe down with the small towel before leaving the bathing area. **Wet floors are the reason for the rule.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Long soaking is better

Long immersion in hot water can be demanding on the body. **Take breaks, drink water, and leave the bath if you feel unwell.** Alcohol before bathing is not advisable.

## On tattoos

Policies vary widely between facilities. **Check in advance; some allow tattoos, some require covering, some do not permit entry.**
