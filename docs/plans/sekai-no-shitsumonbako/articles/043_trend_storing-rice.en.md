# Q. What do people get wrong about storing rice in Japan?｜Japan Questions #43

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/storing-rice` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

If you live in Japan, you will probably buy white rice in large bags. The common assumption is that rice keeps at room temperature indefinitely. The answer from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) is "about one month after milling".

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "rice keeps, so room temperature is fine". MAFF gives about one month after milling as the guide; in heat and humidity, oxidation, mould and insects all advance.

## The guide figures

- MAFF: **the longer it is since milling, the more the taste declines, so about one month is the guide.**
- Industry guidance: **within one month of milling in spring–summer, within two months in autumn–winter.** Eating it **within 45 days** at the longest is recommended.

In hot, humid conditions, **high temperature speeds up oxidation, high humidity encourages mould, and insects become more active.**

> **[Verify]** The one-month guide is based on MAFF's "Kodomo Sōdan" (children's questions) page. **Check the original before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜Leave it in the bag

Retail rice bags often have small air holes. **Moving the rice to a sealable container is better suited.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Under the sink is where rice goes

Under the sink is where damp collects. **The basics are to avoid heat, humidity and direct sunlight.** A cool place such as the vegetable drawer of the fridge is generally described as extending storage compared with room temperature.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Buying in bulk is better value

The longer rice is stored, the more its quality changes. **Buying what you can eat in a month means you end up eating it in better condition.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Old rice is the same once cooked

Flavour changes with time. **You can adjust, for example by changing the amount of water, but it does not go back to how it was.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Insects cannot get in

**Insects are generally described as appearing especially in summer.** Sealing and cool storage are the countermeasures.
