# Q. What do people get wrong about Japanese toner (keshōsui)?｜Japan Questions #46

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/toner` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Japanese toner (化粧水, keshōsui) is talked about as the symbol of Japanese skincare, and there is a bottle in almost every bathroom here. Its role is widely misunderstood. One term from dermatology changes the way you see it.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "toner stores water in the skin". Water given to the stratum corneum evaporates away; what stops it evaporating is oil.

## An index called "transepidermal water loss"

Quite apart from sweat, a small amount of water is constantly evaporating from the skin through the stratum corneum (the outermost layer). This is called **transepidermal water loss (TEWL)**, and **the lower the value, the stronger the barrier function of the stratum corneum**. Comparing TEWL before and after use is one method used in cosmetic evaluation tests.

Moisturising agents fall into two broad groups.

| Group | Typical example | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Water-based** | Glycerin etc. | High affinity with water molecules; **holds** water in the skin |
| **Oil-based** | Petroleum jelly (Vaseline) etc. | Forms an oil film on the skin surface and **physically blocks** evaporation |

Toner is mainly the former. **Its role is to "give"; the role of "not letting go" belongs to the oils in milky lotion (乳液, nyūeki) and cream.**

> **[Verify]** The explanation of TEWL is based on the glossary of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists of Japan (SCCJ) and a pharmaceutical company's material for healthcare professionals. **Check before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜The more you use, the better

Using more does not change how much evaporates. **What matters is not the number of applications but the step that follows.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜It is the same thing as "lotion" in English

Japanese keshōsui is sometimes labelled "lotion" in English, but **it is not the milky, creamy product that word usually suggests to English speakers.** It is a watery liquid.

## Misunderstanding 3｜The moist feeling right after applying is the effect

The feeling right after is the stratum corneum temporarily holding water. **Feel is not the same as result.** The effects that a product may claim as a cosmetic are limited to 56 items by a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare notice (Japan regulates cosmetic claims under the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act, its cosmetics and drugs law).

## Misunderstanding 4｜Using a cotton pad is the correct way

Hands or a cotton pad: **the basic rule is to follow the product's directions.** Not rubbing is described as more important.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Skipping it is not an option

Depending on your skin and the products you combine, reducing the number of steps is also a choice. **More steps does not mean more correct.**
