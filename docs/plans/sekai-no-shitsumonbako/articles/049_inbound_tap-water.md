# Q. What do people get wrong about tap water in Japan?｜Japan Questions #49

Travellers from places where tap water is avoided often assume the same applies here.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that bottled water is necessary. Tap water in Japan is supplied to drinking-water standards set in law.

Water quality is regulated nationally, and suppliers publish their test results.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Nobody drinks from the tap

It is ordinary to drink tap water, use it for cooking, and fill a bottle from it. **Many public facilities provide drinking fountains.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜It is the same everywhere

Source water and treatment differ by area, so taste can differ. **Meeting the standard and tasting identical are not the same thing.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Any tap is drinking water

Taps in some facilities are marked as not for drinking. **Look for the signage; if a tap says it is not potable, it is not.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Hot spring and stream water is fine to drink

Natural water sources are not treated supply. **Do not assume they are safe to drink.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Buying bottled water is pointless

Convenience, temperature and personal preference are all reasonable reasons. **The point is that it is a choice, not a requirement.**
