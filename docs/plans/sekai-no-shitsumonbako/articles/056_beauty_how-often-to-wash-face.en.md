# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about how often to wash your face?｜Japan Questions #56

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/how-often-to-wash-face` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Should you wash in the morning as well? Is water enough? Opinion is divided the world over. Japan, however, has a guide that rests on a guideline from the Japanese Dermatological Association, the country's main professional body for skin doctors.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "the more you wash, the cleaner you are". The Japanese Dermatological Association's guideline recommends washing the face twice a day, and describes over-washing, such as four times a day, as a burden on the skin.

## What the guideline says

The Japanese Dermatological Association's acne guideline recommends **washing twice a day with the lather of soap or a facial cleanser**. Removing sebum helps prevention, but **over-washing, such as four times a day, is reported to burden the skin and lead to dryness or worsening**.

So the guide is "twice". **However, this recommendation is made in the context of acne, and is not meant to apply uniformly to every skin condition.**

> **[Verify]** The above is based on several dermatology clinic websites that present the Japanese Dermatological Association guideline. **Check against the society's original text (the guideline on acne vulgaris) before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜You must use a cleanser in the morning too

The guideline's figure is twice, but what is on your face in the morning is mostly what your own skin has produced. **On days when your skin is not oily, lukewarm water alone is an option.** If your skin feels tight after washing, that is a sign the method is too strong for your skin as it is now.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The more you wash, the cleaner you are

**Four times a day is described as "over-washing".** Removing the sebum you need puts a burden on the way the skin surface works.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Not washing is kinder to the skin

Leaving a lot of sebum in place can also lead to skin trouble. **"Don't wash" is not always the right answer either.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜The same routine all year round

Skin changes with the season and with your physical condition. **You do not need to keep the same number of washes all year.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Changing how often you wash will solve skin trouble

**If acne or eczema persists, do not try to solve it by changing how often you wash.** See a dermatology clinic (covered by Japanese health insurance).
