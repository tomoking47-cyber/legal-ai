# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about caffeine?｜Japan Questions #57

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/caffeine` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Upper limits for caffeine are presented differently from country to country, so conversations about it often talk past each other. Japan's public bodies have taken an unusual route: rather than setting a figure of their own, they present the assessments made overseas. If you have moved to Japan and are looking for "the Japanese limit", this is what you will find.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "Japan has a clear upper limit too". Japan has not set an acceptable daily intake; instead the Food Safety Commission of Japan presents the European assessment (400 mg a day for healthy adults).

## The figures public bodies present

The Food Safety Commission of Japan (Cabinet Office) fact sheet "Caffeine in food" [食品中のカフェイン] (created 2011, updated 2018) presents the following as assessments by overseas bodies:

| Who | Guide |
|---|---|
| Healthy adults | up to **400 mg a day** (5.7 mg/kg at a body weight of 70 kg) |
| During pregnancy | **200 mg a day** or less |
| Children (3–10) and adolescents (10–18) | **3 mg per kg of body weight a day** |

Excess intake is described as possibly causing dizziness, a raised heart rate, agitation, anxiety, tremor and insomnia.

> **[Verify]** The figures are based on the Food Safety Commission fact sheet. **Check against the original PDF before publication. If listing the caffeine content of individual drinks, take those figures from the same document.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜You only need to watch coffee

Caffeine is also in tea, energy drinks and some medicines. **Gyokuro (玉露, a high-grade shaded green tea) is described as containing more than coffee.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Keep to the amount and it is the same for everyone

Constitution, body size, usual intake and any medicines you take all change how you feel it. **400 mg does not mean "safe for everyone".**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Anything before evening has no effect

Metabolism varies from person to person. **For some people, a cup in the early evening affects the night.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Decaf means zero

**Some products still contain small amounts.** Check the label (in Japan, look for カフェインレス, kafein-resu, or デカフェ, dekafe).

## Misunderstanding 5｜During pregnancy you can carry on as usual

**A lower guide of 200 mg a day or less is given for pregnancy. Consult a doctor or pharmacist.**
