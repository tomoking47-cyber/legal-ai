# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about freezing food?｜Japan Questions #63

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/freezing-tips` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

The freezer easily becomes the place where things go "for now". But the quality of frozen food is decided by how fast it freezes. Japanese home freezers are often small and tightly packed, which makes the tips below all the more useful.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "once it is frozen, quality stops changing". If food passes slowly through the −1°C to −5°C band, ice crystals grow large, and liquid (called "drip") leaks out when it thaws.

## The "zone of maximum ice-crystal formation"

When food freezes, **ice crystals grow largest in the band between −1°C and −5°C.** If the food passes through this band slowly, large, sharp crystals damage the cells, and when it thaws, liquid full of flavour (drip) runs out.

Commercial "quick freezing" means taking food through this band **within 30 minutes**. **A home freezer does "slow freezing"**: it cools by air, which takes time, so large crystals form easily.

**What you can do at home is shorten the time spent in that band.** Flatten food thin, lay it on a metal tray, divide it into small portions — all of these are ways of "freezing faster".

> **[Verify]** The temperature band and the 30-minute definition are based on explanations from the refrigeration-equipment industry. **Check against materials from the Japan Frozen Food Association or similar before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜Fast or slow, frozen is frozen

As above, **freezing speed determines how much drip you get.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Putting it in as it is will do

Drying and oxidation start wherever the food touches air. **Press out the air and seal it.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Freezing in one big batch is more efficient

A large block freezes more slowly, and you end up thawing the whole thing every time you use some. **Divide it into single-use portions.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Do not pack the freezer full

Unlike the fridge compartment, **a freezer that is fairly full holds its temperature more steadily**, according to manufacturers' guidance. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's guide is −15°C or below.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Once thawed, it can be refrozen

**Refreezing is not recommended, for reasons of both quality and hygiene.** Thaw only what you will use.
