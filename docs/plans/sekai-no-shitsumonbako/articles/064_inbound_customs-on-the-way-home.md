# Q. What do people get wrong about customs on the way home from Japan?｜Japan Questions #64

Most trip planning covers arriving. The return journey gets far less attention, and that is where things get taken away.

## A. The common misunderstanding is thinking customs is one process. Leaving Japan and entering your own country are two separate sets of rules.

Japan controls export of a few categories. **Your own country controls what you may bring in.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜It is all handled by the airline

Airlines enforce cabin and baggage safety rules. **Customs and quarantine are separate authorities with separate rules.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Duty-free means no limits at home

Buying without Japanese consumption tax says nothing about your own country's allowances. **Duty and allowance limits still apply on arrival.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Declaring things causes problems

Declaring is the process working normally. **Not declaring is what creates problems.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Only food is restricted

Plants, seeds, soil, animal products, some medicines and certain wildlife-derived goods are commonly restricted. **Check before you buy, not after.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Nobody checks

Detector dogs, X-ray screening and random inspection are routine at many borders. **Assume it will be checked.**

## What to do instead

**Look up your destination country's customs and quarantine rules before you shop.** They are published online by every national customs authority. It takes ten minutes and saves the item.
