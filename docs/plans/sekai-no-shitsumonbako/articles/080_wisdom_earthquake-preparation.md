# Q. What do people get wrong about earthquake preparation in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #80

- Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

Preparation here is treated as ordinary household maintenance rather than as an emergency mindset.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that preparation means an emergency bag. Most of it is furniture, water and knowing where to go.

The bag matters. **What is bolted to the wall matters earlier.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜A grab bag is the main thing

Securing tall furniture and appliances so they cannot fall is widely advised. **Injuries often happen before anyone reaches a bag.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Run outside immediately

Guidance from Japanese authorities generally advises protecting your head and staying put until the shaking stops. **Falling objects outside are a hazard.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Supplies means food only

Water, a torch, a radio, a power bank, medication and cash are all commonly listed. **Water is usually the largest item by volume.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Stock it once

Stored food and water have dates. **Rotating stock through normal use is the standard advice.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Visitors do not need to think about it

Knowing your building's evacuation site and having your phone's emergency alerts enabled takes minutes. **Municipal hazard maps are published online.**
