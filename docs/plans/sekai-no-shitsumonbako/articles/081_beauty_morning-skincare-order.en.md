# Q. In what order should I do my morning skincare?｜Japan Questions #81

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/morning-skincare-order` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Should the morning routine differ from the evening one? The debate runs worldwide, but in Japan there is a basic sequence that is widely presented as the standard. If you have just bought a shelf of Japanese products and cannot read the labels, this is the order to start from.

## A. The basic sequence is "cleanse → lotion (化粧水, keshōsui) → milky emulsion (乳液, nyūeki) → sunscreen". But the product's own instructions take priority, and for the final step, sunscreen, it is the amount that decides the performance.

## The basic sequence and the reasoning behind each step

| Step | Action | Reasoning / guide |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cleanse | What is on your face in the morning is mainly your own sebum. **On days when it is not oily, lukewarm water alone is an option** |
| 2 | Water-rich product (化粧水, keshōsui, "lotion") | Gives water to the stratum corneum (outer skin layer). **But it evaporates** |
| 3 | Oil-rich product (乳液, nyūeki, "milky emulsion") | Forms a film on the skin surface and **physically blocks evaporation** |
| 4 | Sunscreen | SPF and PA values are measured with **2 mg per cm²** applied. For the whole face the guide is **0.7–0.9 g** |

This sequence is presented on several medical-institution websites as information from the Japanese Dermatological Association. PA (Protection Grade of UVA) is Japan's UVA rating, shown as PA+ to PA++++.

## Misunderstanding 1｜The order must be followed without exception

**The instructions for each product take priority.** Some products are designed in a way that does not fit this order. Under a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) notice, the efficacy claims cosmetics may make in Japan are limited to 56 items, and a claim that "the order changes the effect" falls outside that list.

## Misunderstanding 2｜More steps are better

Every extra step is another touch on the skin. **Keep to a number you can sustain.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜A little sunscreen spread thinly is enough

**At half the amount, the performance on the label is not achieved.** Of the four steps, this is the only one that belongs to the morning alone. Skimp here and the morning routine loses much of its point.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Keep the same routine all year

Skin changes with humidity and temperature, and Japan's seasons swing from humid summers to dry winters. **There is no need to fix the routine year-round.**
