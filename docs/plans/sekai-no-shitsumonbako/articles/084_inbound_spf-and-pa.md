## A. The common misunderstanding is reading them as one score. SPF and PA measure protection against two different kinds of ultraviolet light.

They are two scales, side by side.

## Misunderstanding 1｜PA is a second opinion on SPF

**SPF relates to UVB. PA relates to UVA.** They are not two measures of the same thing.

## Misunderstanding 2｜More plus signs is always the right choice

PA runs from PA+ to PA++++. **Choose for the situation** — the highest is not automatically the right one for a short errand.

## Misunderstanding 3｜A high SPF covers UVA too

A high SPF number says nothing on its own about UVA. **Look for the PA rating separately.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜The ratings mean the same everywhere

Rating systems and test methods differ between countries. **Do not convert a Japanese rating into a familiar foreign one in your head.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜The rating alone determines the result

The stated performance assumes the stated amount of product. **Applied thinly, a high rating does not behave like one.**
