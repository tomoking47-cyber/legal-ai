# Q. In what order should I do my evening skincare?｜Japan Questions #86

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/night-skincare-order` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Evening routines tend to gain extra steps, and that is where most of the arguments about order gather. Japanese skincare follows a fairly fixed sequence, and products in a Japanese drugstore (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.) are labelled by their role in it. Here is the basic order and the reasoning behind each step.

## A. The basic pattern is "makeup remover (クレンジング, kurenjingu) → face wash (洗顔, sengan) → lotion (化粧水, keshōsui) → emulsion (乳液, nyūeki)". What marks out the evening is the "take off" step, and whether you need it depends on what you put on your face that day.

## The basic sequence, and the basis for each step

| Step | Stage | Basis / guide |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Take off (makeup remover — oil, balm or milk) | Removes oil-based dirt (makeup, sunscreen). **Not essential on a day you used neither** |
| 2 | Wash (face wash) | The Japanese Dermatological Association guideline gives **twice a day** as the guide for face washing. **Four times a day is over-washing** |
| 3 | The water-rich product (lotion, 化粧水) | Gives water to the stratum corneum (the outermost layer of the skin) |
| 4 | The oil-rich product (emulsion, 乳液) | Forms a film on the skin surface and **limits transepidermal water loss** (water evaporating out through the skin) |

This sequence is presented as Japanese Dermatological Association information on several medical-clinic websites.

## Misunderstanding 1｜The take-off step is essential every day

Makeup removers are made for oil-based dirt. **On a day you used neither sunscreen nor makeup, there is no need to use something of the same strength.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜The longer you spend, the better it works

The longer a product is worked over the skin, the more friction the skin takes. **Keep to the time and amount on the product label.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜"No double cleansing needed" means never use a face wash

"W洗顔不要" (daburu sengan fuyō, "no double cleansing needed") is a statement about how that product is designed. **Depending on the sunscreen you used and the state of your skin, a face wash may still suit you better.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜You need the same number of products as in the morning

More steps at night, fewer in the morning — **or the other way round: there is no rule.** Under Japanese law the claims a cosmetic may make are limited to a list of 56 items, and "more steps means more effect" is not among them.
