# Q. What do people get wrong about airing futons in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #95

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/futon-airing` ／ Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

On a clear day you will see bedding hanging from balconies across a whole street.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that it is about sunshine. It is mostly about removing the moisture a sleeping body puts into the bedding overnight.

Sun helps. **Dry air does the work.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜It is about disinfecting in sunlight

The practical purpose is drying. **Bedding absorbs moisture every night; airing releases it.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Longer in the sun is better

Extended strong sun can degrade fabric and colour. **A few hours in the middle of the day is the usual guidance.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Beating it hard is part of the process

Vigorous beating can damage the filling. **Light tapping, or simply airing, is now the more common advice.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Any day will do

Airing on a humid day adds moisture instead of removing it. **The weather decides the day, not the schedule.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜It only applies to traditional futons

Mattresses, duvets and pillows all take on moisture. **Where balconies are unavailable, indoor airing racks and dryers do the same job.**
