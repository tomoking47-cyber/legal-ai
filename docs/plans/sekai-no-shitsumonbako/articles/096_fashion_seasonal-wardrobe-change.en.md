# Q. In what order should I do the seasonal wardrobe change (衣替え)?｜Japan Questions #96

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/seasonal-wardrobe-change` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Koromogae (衣替え) is the Japanese custom of swapping the wardrobe over between seasons. It is usually thought of as a swapping job. In practice it is a job of preparing clothes for storage — and in a climate as humid as Japan's, that preparation decides what you find when you open the box.

## A. The basic order is "wash → dry completely → sort → store away from moisture". The state of your clothes when you put them away decides the state they are in when you take them out.

## The basic sequence, and the basis for each step

| Step | Stage | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wash everything you are putting away | Invisible sebum and sweat cause yellowing and odour during storage |
| 2 | Dry completely | Sealed away damp, **bacteria start to multiply in about 5 hours** (findings on the bacteria behind the "indoor-drying smell", 部屋干し臭, of laundry dried indoors) |
| 3 | Check for damage and fit; decide what to let go | Saves storage space |
| 4 | Put away on a low-humidity day, with air able to circulate | Do not seal moisture in with the clothes |

For washing, follow **the care label (Japan uses the international JIS L0001 symbols, in force since December 2016; the August 2024 revision brought the set to 43 symbols).**

> **[Verify]** The care-label system is from Consumer Affairs Agency material. **Check before publication.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜If it looks clean, it need not be washed

Invisible sebum and sweat remain. **They are generally described as the cause of yellowing and odour during storage.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜You can put things away on a rainy day

Seal things up on a humid day and you seal in the moisture too. **Choose a dry day.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Vacuum-compression bags save space with no downside

Some materials lose their shape. **Check the care label.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜More moth repellent works better

**Some moth repellents (防虫剤, bōchūzai) cannot be used together.** Check the label and keep to the stated amount.

## Misunderstanding 5｜The wardrobe change is fixed at twice a year

The climate is changing. **There is no need to fix the number of times or the timing.**
