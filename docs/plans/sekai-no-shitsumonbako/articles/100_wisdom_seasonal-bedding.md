# Q. What do people get wrong about seasonal bedding in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #100

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/seasonal-bedding` ／ Status: **Draft — legal review pending**

Bedding here changes with the season in a way that is closer to changing clothes than to buying furniture.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that bedding is a fixed set. It is treated as seasonal equipment that is swapped, aired and stored.

The bed is not a permanent object. **In many homes, it is assembled each night and put away each morning.**

## Misunderstanding 1｜One duvet covers the year

Weights are changed with the season, and layers are added or removed. **The base does not change; the covering does.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Storage is the problem

Bedding that folds away is a solution to small rooms, not a burden. **The floor becomes usable again during the day.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Summer bedding is just thinner

Materials matter as much as thickness — woven mats, cool-touch covers and reed pads are all used. **The aim is moving heat and moisture away, not only reducing weight.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Storing it is just folding it

Bedding is dried before storage, and stored somewhere ventilated. **Moisture stored with it is the thing that causes problems later.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜It is only tradition

The reasons are climatic: humid summers, dry winters, and rooms that serve more than one purpose. **The habit follows the conditions.**
