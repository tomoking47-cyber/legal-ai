# Q. What do people get wrong about seasonal bedding in Japan?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #100

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/seasonal-bedding` ／ Status: **Draft — sources unverified**

Bedding here changes with the season in a way that is closer to changing clothes than to buying furniture. The reasons are climatic and measurable.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that bedding is a fixed set. It is seasonal equipment chosen for how it handles moisture, in a climate where summer humidity sits above the 60% threshold at which dust mites thrive.

## Why the material changes

| Season | Typical choice | Property |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | **Linen (asa), cotton, woven reed (igusa) pads** | Absorb and release moisture quickly (吸放湿性) |
| Spring / autumn | Mid-weight quilt plus a towel blanket | Layers adjustable for large day-to-day swings |
| Winter | **Down (umou) duvet** with a blanket | Traps air; light for its warmth |

The variable being managed is **moisture.** Dust mites thrive above **60% humidity**; summer air routinely exceeds it. Materials that pull moisture away and release it fast are the summer answer. Airing removes what the night put in (→ #95).

## Misunderstanding 1｜One duvet covers the year

Weights and materials change with the season. **The base does not change; the covering does.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Storage is the problem

Bedding that folds away is a solution to small rooms — the average home is 90.86 m². **The floor becomes usable again during the day.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Summer bedding is just thinner

**Material matters as much as thickness.** The aim is moving heat and moisture away, not only reducing weight.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Storing it is just folding it

Bedding is dried before storage and stored somewhere ventilated. **Moisture stored with it causes the problems later.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜It is only tradition

The reasons are climatic: humid summers, dry winters, and rooms that serve more than one purpose. **The habit follows the conditions.**
