# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about washing sneakers?｜Japan Questions #103

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/washing-sneakers` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

"Can I put my sneakers in the washing machine?" comes up again and again on Q&A sites in every country. In Japan, coin laundries (コインランドリー) have started installing dedicated shoe-washing machines, so the idea that sneakers are "washable" has spread. Whether yours are depends on what they are made of.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "all sneakers are washed the same way". The method depends on the material (canvas, synthetic leather, real leather, mesh), and real leather and glued parts do not take well to water.

## A guide by material

| Material | Water-wash | Suggested method | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canvas (cotton) | Yes | Brush with a neutral (中性) detergent, or machine-wash in a shoe laundry bag | Coloured canvas may fade |
| Mesh (polyester, etc.) | Yes | As above | High-heat drying deforms it |
| Synthetic leather | Surface only | Wipe with a damp cloth | Long soaking makes it peel |
| Real leather / suede | No | Dedicated cleaner | Water makes it stiffen and discolour |

Shoes for household use are not required to carry care labels the way clothing is. **If the maker has provided care instructions, they are the only official basis.** Otherwise, decide from the material.

## Misunderstanding 1｜The machine will wreck them

Canvas and mesh are generally described as machine-washable at home if you use **a shoe laundry bag, a gentle cycle and cold water**. Shoes can scratch the drum and make noise, however, and **some washing-machine makers prohibit shoes in their instruction manuals.** Check yours first.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Bleach will make them white again

Yellowing of a rubber sole is **oxidation of the rubber**. Bleach does not reverse it well, and chlorine bleach damages rubber and adhesives. **Handle the upper and the sole as two different materials.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Speed things up in the tumble dryer

Heat can **loosen the adhesive and make the sole come away**. The basic method is to **stuff the shoes with newspaper or kitchen paper and dry them in a well-ventilated, shaded spot**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Direct sunlight disinfects them

UV light **speeds up yellowing and fading**. If hygiene is the aim, **washing properly and drying completely** matters more.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Once washed, nothing more is needed

A **waterproofing spray** (防水スプレー, bōsui supurē) applied after drying helps dirt stay off, but **it must be used outdoors, without breathing it in**. The National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan has issued warnings about health harm from inhaling fluorine-based waterproofing sprays.
