# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about skipping breakfast?｜Japan Questions #125

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/skipping-breakfast` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Whether to skip breakfast, and whether intermittent fasting is healthy, is debated in every country. In Japan, the "breakfast-skipping rate" is an item in the national health survey and has been tracked for many years. Here is what the statistics show and how people in Japan think about it.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is that skipping breakfast on its own makes you lose weight. The national survey shows that skipping breakfast is most common among younger people, and it is discussed alongside disrupted daily rhythms. At the same time, it is also true that there is no need to force yourself to eat. It is a judgement about your daily life as a whole.

## In numbers

| Item | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast-skipping rate (men in their 20s) | **About 30%** | National Health and Nutrition Survey (2023) |
| Breakfast-skipping rate (women in their 20s) | **About 20%** | Same |
| Breakfast-skipping rate (men, age 20 and over) | **About 17%** | Same |
| Related Health Japan 21 (third term) target | Fewer people skipping breakfast (younger generations) | MHLW |

The survey definition of "skipping" includes **eating nothing, eating only sweets or fruit, and taking only tablets or supplements**.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Skipping breakfast is bad for you, full stop

**It depends on your own daily rhythm and how much you ate the night before.** The national target of "fewer people skipping breakfast" exists because skipping tends to go together with **a night-owl lifestyle, late dinners and disrupted habits**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Skip it and you lose weight

Skipping breakfast is generally described as making **lunch and dinner portions grow**, so there is no assurance that total intake falls. **Body weight is decided by the day's total.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Breakfast only counts if it is a proper meal

Even **a banana, a yoghurt or a bowl of miso soup** counts. Eating something in the morning is generally described as **playing a part in resetting the body clock** (the Sleep Guide 2023 mentions morning light and regular breakfast times).

## Misunderstanding 4｜A sweet pastry and juice is a fine quick breakfast

A breakfast of sugar alone is generally described as **making you hungrier during the morning**. The standard practice among people living in Japan is to **add a little protein (egg, tofu, dairy)**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Children are fine without breakfast

The **National Assessment of Academic Ability** (全国学力・学習状況調査) by the Ministry of Education (MEXT) consistently shows that pupils who eat breakfast every day tend to score higher. **This is a correlation, not proof of cause,** but it is one reason the government keeps running its "Early to bed, early to rise, and eat breakfast" (早寝早起き朝ごはん) campaign.
