# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about Japan's annual health check?｜Japan Questions #130

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/annual-health-check` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

In Japan an annual health check (健康診断, kenkō shindan, often shortened to kenshin) is a normal part of life for company employees. Many countries have no culture of yearly check-ups, so visitors and foreign residents are often surprised when their employer books one for them. Here is how the system works and what your part in it is.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is that the health check is the company's obligation and nothing to do with you. Under Japan's Industrial Safety and Health Act, the employer has a duty to provide it, and the employee has a duty to attend. For ages 40–74 there is also the Specific Health Check (the so-called "metabolic check"), with an uptake rate of about 60%.

## How the system works

| Scheme | Who | Legal basis | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General health check (regular check)** | Regularly employed workers. **Once a year** | Industrial Safety and Health Act, Article 66 | Paid by the employer |
| **Specific Health Check (特定健診, tokutei kenshin)** | Health-insurance members aged **40–74** | Act on Assurance of Medical Care for Elderly People | Paid by the insurer (free to a small co-payment) |
| **Cancer screening** | Run by municipalities (stomach, lung, colorectal, breast, cervical) | Health Promotion Act | Free to a few thousand yen, depending on the municipality |
| Ningen dock (comprehensive private check-up) | Voluntary | — | Self-paid (subsidy schemes exist) |

- **Uptake of the Specific Health Check is about 59%** (fiscal 2022, MHLW). The national target is 70%.
- The Industrial Safety and Health Act places **a duty to provide on the employer and a duty to attend on the worker** (there is no penalty on the worker's side).

## Misunderstanding 1｜No symptoms, no need to go

The purpose of the check is **to spot changes before symptoms appear**. Most lifestyle-related diseases have no noticeable symptoms in their early stages.

## Misunderstanding 2｜A result marked "re-examination needed" can wait and see

"Re-examination needed" (要再検査) and "detailed examination needed" (要精密検査) are **instructions to go back and be examined again**. Ignore them and the check was pointless. **If you do not know where to go, ask the organisation that ran the check** — that is the quickest route.

## Misunderstanding 3｜You can eat and drink normally the day before

Many items (blood sugar, triglycerides) assume **10 hours or more of fasting**. Drinking or overeating the night before **distorts the results** and is a common cause of re-examination.

## Misunderstanding 4｜A figure "within the reference range" means no problem at all

The reference range is **a statistical range derived from the distribution across many people**. It is not an assurance of "no abnormality". The value of the check lies in **watching the trend year by year**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Self-employed and freelance people cannot get a health check

**Members of National Health Insurance (国民健康保険, kokumin kenkō hoken) are also eligible for the Specific Health Check from age 40.** Your municipality posts you a voucher (受診券). **Under 40, some municipalities and insurers run their own checks.**
