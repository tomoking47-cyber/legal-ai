# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about sheet masks?｜Japan Questions #161

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/sheet-masks` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Sheet masks spread from Japan and Korea to the rest of the world, and "can I use one every day?" and "how long do I leave it on?" are asked constantly in overseas communities. In Japan, what a cosmetic is allowed to claim is fixed by law, so a mask sold here can promise less than its marketing abroad might suggest. Here is how people in Japan actually use them.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "the longer you leave it on, the deeper it penetrates." Once the sheet starts to dry, moisture moves back out of your skin into the sheet. The basic rule is to follow the time on the pack (usually 5–15 minutes). And for cosmetics in Japan, "penetration" is defined as reaching no further than the stratum corneum, the outermost layer of the skin.

## What to base your decision on

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Time on the skin | **Follow the product label.** Usually 5–15 minutes. **Do not leave it on until it dries** |
| Frequency | Some products are **for daily use**, others **for once or twice a week**. Check the label |
| What "penetrates" means | In cosmetics advertising, "penetrates" may only mean **as far as the stratum corneum** (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare notice; industry advertising guidelines) |
| Storage | Away from heat and direct sunlight. **Keep in the fridge only if the label says so** |
| After opening | Use a single-sachet mask in one go. For multi-sheet packs, **keep the opening clean and use them up soon** |

- The effects a cosmetic may claim are limited to **56 items** (for example "moisturises the skin", "tightens the skin") under the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act (Japan's cosmetics and drugs law). **"Makes dark spots disappear" or "gets rid of pores" cannot be said.**
- The Japan Cosmetic Industry Association (JCIA) **Guidelines for Appropriate Advertising of Cosmetics** require the note **"as far as the stratum corneum"** whenever "penetrates" is used.

> **[Verify]** Check the 56 items against MHLW notice Yakushoku-hatsu 0721 No. 1, and the "penetration" footnote against the latest edition of the JCIA Guidelines for Appropriate Advertising of Cosmetics.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Use one every day and your skin will change

Daily-use products exist, but **the effect stays within the cosmetic range (moisture and protection).** The starting rule is: **stop if your skin reacts.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Leaving it on until it dries wastes less

A sheet that has begun to dry **draws moisture out of your skin.** People in Japan take the mask off at the stated time and use the liquid left in the sachet on the neck and hands.

## Misunderstanding 3｜You need not put anything on afterwards

A sheet mask plays the role of **toner to serum**. The usual next step is **a milky lotion (乳液, nyūeki) or cream on top** to stop the water evaporating (see our article on skincare order).

## Misunderstanding 4｜Chilling it in the fridge tightens pores

A cold mask **feels refreshing for a moment**, but **pores do not get smaller.** In a product not designed for fridge storage, ingredients can separate or condensation can form, changing its quality.

## Misunderstanding 5｜The more expensive, the more concentrated the ingredients

Price reflects **ingredients, packaging and brand** together. **The ingredient list is in descending order of quantity**, so looking at what comes first is a better comparison (see our article on reading ingredient labels).
