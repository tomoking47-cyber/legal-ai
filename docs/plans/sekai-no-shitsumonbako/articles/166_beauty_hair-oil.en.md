# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about using hair oil?｜Japan Questions #166

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/hair-oil` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Hair oil has become a staple worldwide, but "when, where and how much" can turn the result from glossy to greasy. In Japan it is sold as a type of leave-in conditioner (洗い流さないトリートメント, arainagasanai torītomento), and the way to use it is fairly settled. This article sets out that approach, and what a hair oil may legally claim here.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "the more you put on, the shinier it gets." Too much leaves hair heavy and greasy, and on the scalp it can clog pores. The guide is: on towel-dried, damp hair, 1–2 drops (short hair) to 3–4 drops (long hair), concentrated on the ends.

## Guidelines

| Hair length | Amount (guide) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Short | **1 drop to half a pump** | Ends |
| Medium | **2–3 drops (1 pump)** | Ends to mid-lengths |
| Long | **3–4 drops (1–2 pumps)** | Ends to mid-lengths |

| When | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **After towel-drying, before the hair dryer** | Protects the hair surface from heat and friction (the main use) |
| Finishing a style | Shine and taming frizz. **A very small amount** |
| Dryness or static during the day | A little on the ends |

- **Spread and warm it well between your palms**, then apply in the order **ends → mid-lengths → surface.** **Do not apply to the scalp or roots.**
- Hair oil is a **cosmetic**. The effects it may claim are within the 56 items, such as **"gives hair body and resilience", "makes hair supple" and "supplies and retains moisture and oil in the hair".** **Wording such as "repairs hair" cannot be used for a cosmetic** under the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act (Japan's cosmetics and drugs law).

> **[Verify]** Check the 56 items against MHLW notice Yakushoku-hatsu 0721 No. 1. Follow the product label for amounts.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It is pointless on wet hair

**Towel-dried, damp hair is the main place to use it.** It is applied to protect the surface from the heat and friction of the dryer.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Putting it on the scalp moisturises it

It causes **clogged pores and greasiness.** Scalp products are a separate category (scalp lotions and the like).

## Misunderstanding 3｜More oil means softer hair

**It weighs hair down, attracts dust, and what is not washed out stains the pillow.** Start small and add.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Hair oil restores damaged hair

Hair is made of **dead cells**, and damaged parts do not recover. Oil **coats the surface to improve feel and shine**; legally, too, it cannot be called "repair".

## Misunderstanding 5｜Hair oil can be used on the skin as well

**Avoid using on the skin any product not labelled for face or body.** The fragrance and other ingredients are formulated differently. Follow the use stated on the label.
