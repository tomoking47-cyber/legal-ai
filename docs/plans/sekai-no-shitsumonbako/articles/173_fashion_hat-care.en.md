# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about caring for hats?｜Japan Questions #173

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/hat-care` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

"Can I wash a hat, and how do I stop it losing its shape?" is asked in every country. In Japan hats are worn a great deal, against strong summer UV and the winter cold, so tips on sweat stains and storage are widely shared. Here is what people here go by.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "hats cannot be washed". It depends on the material and the care label. Cotton and polyester caps can often be hand-washed; straw, felt and leather are wiped rather than washed. Two rules apply to all of them: "deal with sweat stains quickly" and "shape the hat while it dries".

## Guide by material

| Material | Washable? | Method | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cotton or polyester (caps) | **Hand-wash if the label allows** | Press-wash in neutral detergent; **do not put the brim in the washing machine** | The brim's core (card or plastic) deforms |
| Straw or raffia | No | Remove dust with a dry cloth or soft brush. **Dab sweat with a firmly wrung cloth** | Wet, it deforms and moulds |
| Felt (wool) | No | Brush dust off along the nap. **Some people reshape with steam** | Water shrinks it and leaves stains |
| Knitted beanie | **Depends on the label** | Same as washing knitwear (press-wash, dry flat) | Stretching |
| Leather or synthetic leather | No | Wipe. Cream for real leather | Hydrolysis (synthetic leather) |

- Hats also fall under the **Household Goods Quality Labelling Act** (as textile products), so they carry **a material label and a care label**. Look at **the label on the sweatband (the tape inside the crown)**.
- **Sweat stains are salt and skin oil.** Left alone they yellow the fabric or leave a white ring. **When you get home, dab the band with a firmly wrung cloth and dry in the shade.**

> **[Verify]** Check the scope of the labelling requirement in the Consumer Affairs Agency's "Textile Products Quality Labelling Regulations".

## Misunderstanding 1｜A cap can go in the washing machine whole

**The brim's core deforms or breaks.** **Hand-wash, and do not soak the brim too much.** Some people use a cap-washing cage (a frame that holds the hat's shape).

## Misunderstanding 2｜The white ring is dirt and will rub off

**It is sweat salt that has dried and risen to the surface.** Rubbing damages the fabric. **Press with a damp towel to dissolve the salt out, then dry in the shade.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Hang it up to dry

A wet hat **loses its shape under its own weight**. The standard method is to **set it over a bowl or a rolled towel to form the shape, and dry in the shade**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜A straw hat is only for summer, so it needs no care

**Sweat and damp bring mould.** At the end of the season, **remove dust, dab the sweat areas, and store in a breathable box with a desiccant**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Felt hats stand up to rain

**They shrink when wet and stains remain.** If one gets wet, **press with a towel, reshape it and let it dry naturally**. **A hairdryer causes shrinkage.**
