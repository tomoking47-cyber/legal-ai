# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about folding clothes and choosing hangers?｜Japan Questions #176

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/folding-and-hangers` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

"Folding clothes to stand upright" is the best-known of the tidying methods that spread from Japan to the rest of the world. Together with how to choose a hanger, here is the know-how for keeping clothes undamaged in a small Japanese flat.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "hanging is kinder to clothes". Knitwear and jersey tops stretch under their own weight when hung, and the shoulders poke out. The basics are to divide "clothes to hang" from "clothes to fold" by material, and to match the hanger to the garment's shoulder width.

## Hang or fold

| Hang | Fold |
|---|---|
| Shirts and blouses, jackets, coats, trousers, dresses that crease easily | **Knitwear and jumpers**, T-shirts and jersey tops, denim, sportswear, underwear and socks |
| Keeps the shape, avoids creases | **No stretching from weight**, and more fits in |

### Choosing a hanger

| Garment | Hanger | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jackets and coats | **Wooden or thick plastic with broad shoulders**, the same width as the garment's shoulders | Keeps the shoulder line |
| Shirts | Slim, with rounded shoulders | No marks on the shoulders |
| Trousers | Clip type, or **with a bar** (clamp the hem) | Keeps the crease |
| Avoid | **Thin metal wire hangers** (long term) | Shoulder marks, rust, deformation |

- Upright folding has two advantages: **everything in the drawer is visible**, and **no weight rests on the clothes underneath**.
- Fill drawers to **80% or less**. Overstuffing causes creases and damp.

> **[Verify]** The hanger-width guides are general figures based on guidance from apparel and storage-goods makers.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Knitwear creases less if it is hung

**Hanging stretches the shoulders and neck.** Fold knitwear. If you really must hang it, **fold it in half over the hanger's bar**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The dry cleaner's wire hangers are good enough

Fine for a short time, but **long term they mark the shoulders, and in damp the rust can transfer to the clothes.** The basic rule is **when clothes come back, remove the plastic and change the hanger** (the plastic traps damp).

## Misunderstanding 3｜The bigger the hanger, the better it supports the garment

**Wider than the shoulders, the sleeve heads poke out and get marked.** Match the garment's shoulder width.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Folded clothes collapse when you pull one out

**Stored upright**, the neighbours hold each other up when one is removed. The trick is to **make the folded heights uniform to match the depth of the drawer**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Vacuum bags tidy everything away

**Long compression damages the springiness of down, wool and knitwear.** Keep vacuum bags for **short-term storage of off-season cotton items and bedding**, and **air the contents after opening** before use.
