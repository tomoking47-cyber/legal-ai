# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about caring for umbrellas?｜Japan Questions #177

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/umbrella-care` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Japan is one of the world's biggest consumers of umbrellas: around 120–130 million a year, many of them the clear plastic kind (ビニール傘, binīru-gasa) sold at every convenience store. Here is the know-how for keeping an umbrella out of the bin, and how its water repellency can be brought back.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "when the water stops beading, the umbrella is finished". In most cases the water-repellent finish is still on the fabric; the surface is dirty and the fibre's fine pile has flattened. Dry it, warm it with a hairdryer, then use repellent spray, in that order, and it usually comes back. If no rib is broken, it can be fixed.

- **Folding it while wet** causes **mould, rust and smell**. **Open it and dry it in the shade.**
- **Rib repairs**: many can be done with parts from a 100-yen shop or by an umbrella repair shop, and **some municipalities and shops run umbrella-repair workshops**.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Umbrellas are consumables

**An umbrella with solid ribs and fabric can last 10 years with care.** The mass disposal of plastic umbrellas is raised as a problem in Ministry of the Environment material too.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Lost repellency means the fabric has degraded

**Dirt and flattened pile** are the main cause. First **dry it and use a hairdryer on warm**. If water still does not bead, repellent spray.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Fold a wet umbrella and put it in the stand

**Closed while wet, the inside never dries: mould, rust and smell.** **Open it in the entrance hall to dry.** If you want to dry it in the shared areas of a block of flats, check the building's rules first.

## Misunderstanding 4｜An umbrella cannot be washed with water

**You can rinse the fabric and wash it lightly with a sponge and diluted neutral detergent.** **Rubbing removes the repellent finish**, so only press. **Wipe rust on the ribs with a cloth and dry.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜A broken rib means the bin

**A single broken rib can be fixed with a splice piece (a repair part).** The parts are sold at 100-yen shops and DIY stores, and **some shops and repair events take umbrellas**.
