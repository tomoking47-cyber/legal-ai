# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about snoring?｜Japan Questions #182

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/snoring` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Snoring is often dismissed as nothing more than a noise problem. In Japan, however, the number of people with undiagnosed sleep apnoea syndrome (SAS) is estimated in the millions, and public information on it is growing. Here we set out the everyday measures, and the line at which you should see a doctor.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "snoring is just how some people are built, and has nothing to do with health." Most snoring is the sound of air passing through a narrowed airway. If it comes with pauses in breathing, strong daytime sleepiness or a headache on waking, sleep apnoea is possible, and that is a matter for a doctor. On the lifestyle side, the basics are sleeping on your side, losing weight and avoiding alcohol before bed.

## What to base your judgement on

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| How snoring happens | During sleep **the tongue and soft palate drop and the airway narrows**; the air makes the tissue vibrate and produce sound |
| Sleep apnoea syndrome (SAS) | **Breathing stops for 10 seconds or more, 5 or more times an hour** during sleep. Undiagnosed cases in Japan are estimated at **several million** |
| When to see a doctor | **You are told your breathing stops / strong daytime sleepiness / headache or dry mouth on waking / getting up to urinate at night** |
| Tests | **A simple test you can do at home** (covered by Japanese health insurance) → an overnight hospital test if needed |
| Management | **CPAP (continuous positive airway pressure therapy)**, a mouthpiece, lifestyle changes and so on. **The doctor decides** |

| Everyday measure | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sleep on your side** (body pillow, or a cushion behind your back) | On your back, the tongue drops back more easily |
| **Avoid alcohol before bed** | Alcohol relaxes the muscles and narrows the airway |
| **Manage your weight** | Fat around the neck narrows the airway |
| **Deal with a blocked nose** (hay fever, dry air) | Mouth breathing makes snoring louder |
| Re-check your **pillow height** | Too high, and the airway bends (see our pillow article) |

## Misunderstanding 1｜Snoring proves you are sleeping deeply

It is **the sound of forcing breath through a narrowed airway**. People who snore loudly are generally described as often having **poorer sleep quality**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Slim people do not snore

Many Japanese people have **a small jaw and a narrow airway by build**, and **it is well known that slim people develop SAS too**. Weight alone is no guide.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Anti-snoring gadgets solve it

Nasal dilator strips and mouth tape can help with **some mild snoring**, but **where breathing stops, it is a medical matter**. Masking it with gadgets delays detection.

## Misunderstanding 4｜A child's snoring is just cute

Children's snoring can be caused by **enlarged adenoids or tonsils**, and it can **affect growth and concentration**. **If it continues, see a paediatrician or an ENT (ear, nose and throat) clinic.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Testing means a hospital stay and a lot of trouble

**A simple overnight test at home, with sensors on a finger and at the nose**, is available under health insurance. Ask at **an internal medicine clinic (内科, naika), an ENT clinic (耳鼻科, jibika) or a sleep clinic (睡眠外来)**.
