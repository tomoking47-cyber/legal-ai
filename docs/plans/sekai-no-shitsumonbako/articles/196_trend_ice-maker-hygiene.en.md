# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about fridge ice-makers and ice hygiene?｜Japan Questions #196

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/ice-maker-hygiene` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

An automatic ice-maker is standard on Japanese fridges, but it draws many queries: "I don't know how to clean it" and "the ice smells." Ice is often assumed to be safe because it is frozen — a misunderstanding found in Q&A forums abroad too.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "ice is frozen, so bacteria cannot grow." Freezing stops bacteria multiplying, but it does not kill them. The water tank, the water channel and the ice tray are where slime and mould form. Makers' guidance is to wash the tank weekly and clean the ice-making channel about once a month (it depends on the model).

## Care guide (what most makers advise)

| Part | How often | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Water tank** | **Once a week** (change the water daily) | Wash with detergent, wash the filter too. **Dry before putting back** |
| Water channel and ice tray | **Once a month to once every few months** (the model's "ice-maker clean" function) | Use the built-in cleaning function or a cleaning agent (**citric-acid type** is usually specified) |
| Ice bin | Once a month | Wash and dry. **Throw away old ice** |
| Filter | **Replace every 3–4 years** (depends on the model) | Replacement interval in the manual |

- The tank should normally be filled with **tap water**. **Mineral water and filtered water contain no chlorine, so slime and mould form more easily.** Even for models that allow them, makers advise **washing the tank more often**.
- The causes of **smelly ice** are **slime in the tank, smells transferred from food in the freezer, and ice left too long**. The guide is to **use ice within 2–3 days**.

> **[Verify]** Check cleaning intervals and permitted types of water in the instruction manual for your own fridge.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Ice is frozen, so it is clean

**Freezing does not kill bacteria.** Bacteria and mould that grew in the tank or channel **come out mixed into the ice.** Handle ice as **a food, just like water**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Ice from mineral water tastes better and is safer

**Without chlorine, bacteria grow more easily inside the tank**, and makers **recommend tap water**. Minerals can also clog the filter.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Just topping up the tank is enough

**Adding water to what is left** causes slime. The rule is **replace all the water daily and wash the tank weekly**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜The ice tray is out of sight, so it needs no cleaning

**Many models have an "ice-maker clean" mode** that flushes the channel with **a specified cleaner such as citric acid**. Many models say **do not use chlorine bleach** (it damages the parts), so check the manual.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Cloudy white ice means dirt

That is **dissolved air and minerals crystallising**, not dirt. **A smell or black specks** may mean dirt or mould, and is the signal to clean.
