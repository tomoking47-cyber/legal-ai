# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about eye make-up hygiene?｜Japan Questions #222

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/eye-makeup-hygiene` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Mascara and eyeliner are used right next to the eye, the most delicate mucous membrane on the body. "How long can I keep it?" and "Can I share it?" come up constantly in expat forums. In Japan, eye problems linked to make-up are reported to eye doctors and to the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "cosmetics keep for three years, so mascara keeps for three years too." Three years is the guide for an unopened product. Once opened, the general guide is about 3 months for mascara and 3–6 months for liquid eyeliner. Products that touch the eye's mucous membrane grow bacteria easily, and the basic rule is not to share or lend them.

## Guide after opening

| Product | Guide after opening | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Mascara** | **3 months** | The brush carries air and bacteria in every time it goes in and out |
| Liquid eyeliner | **3–6 months** | Same as above |
| Pencil eyeliner | **About 1 year** (sharpen before use) | Sharpening renews the surface |
| Powder eyeshadow | **1–2 years** | Dry, so bacteria grow slowly. **Wash the applicators and brushes** |
| Cream eyeshadow | **6 months–1 year** | Touched with the fingers |
| False-eyelash glue | **6 months after opening**; follow the label | Many reports of dermatitis |

- Japanese cosmetics that **stay stable for 3 years or more unopened are not required to carry an expiry date** (Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act — Japan's cosmetics and drugs law), so most products show no date. **After opening is a different matter.**
- "Tightlining" (インライン, in-rain) — drawing liner on the inner rim inside the lash line, where the **meibomian glands** are — is sometimes warned against by eye doctors as a **cause of blocked glands and dry eye**.
- **Eyelash extensions may legally be applied only by a licensed beautician (美容師, biyōshi)** (MHLW notice, 2008). Eye injuries from unlicensed application have been reported to the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan.

> **[Verify]** The after-opening guides are general figures based on maker and Japan Cosmetic Industry Association guidance. Check the licence requirement for eyelash extensions against the MHLW notice.

## Misunderstanding 1｜If it smells and looks the same, it is fine to use

**Bacterial growth in mascara cannot be seen.** **Replace at 3 months**, or sooner **if it clumps or starts to dry out**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Dried-out mascara can be thinned with water or skin lotion

**Adding water makes bacteria grow faster, and the ingredients of a skin lotion are not designed to go in the eye.** When it dries out, replace it.

## Misunderstanding 3｜It is fine to use a tester or a friend's mascara

It can **cause eye infections such as conjunctivitis**. **Do not share eye make-up**, and at the counter use **disposable brushes and applicators** on testers.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Tightlining is the Japanese eye look

**Liner on the inner rim of the lashes can block the glands and cause dry, inflamed eyes.** Drawing it **outside the lash line** is the safer choice.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Lash glue is a cosmetic, so it is safe

The main ingredient of the glue is **cyanoacrylate**, and there are reports of **dermatitis, red eyes and corneal damage**. **Patch-test it, and have extensions applied by a licensed professional.** If anything goes wrong, see an eye doctor (眼科, ganka) straight away.
