# Q. What do people get wrong about the rainy season (tsuyu) for travellers?｜Japan Questions #262

- Shelf: `inbound` ／ URL: `/en/questions/packing-for-the-rainy-season` ／ Status: **Draft — sources unverified**

June to mid-July is the cheapest time to visit Japan and the most misunderstood: "rainy season" suggests monsoon downpours all day. The reality is grey humidity punctuated by rain — and a few things that make it pleasant.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that it rains continuously. Tokyo averages about 12 rainy days in June, roughly the same count as March, but with about twice the total rainfall and humidity near 75–80%. Pack for humidity rather than rain: quick-dry fabrics, a compact umbrella, breathable shoes, and expect hair and skin to behave differently.

## The numbers (Tokyo, June normals)

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Days with ≥1 mm rain | **about 12** (March: about 11) |
| Total rainfall | **about 168 mm** (March: about 116 mm) |
| Relative humidity | **75–80%** |
| Daily high / low | **26°C / 19°C** |
| Rainy season dates (Kanto, average) | **about 7 June to 19 July** |
| Hokkaido | **No rainy season** |
| Okinawa | Earlier: **early May to late June** |

- Rain often comes as **long drizzle or a few hours of heavy rain**, not all day every day. Clear, hot days occur between fronts.
- **Hydrangeas** are at their peak; **temple gardens** are at their greenest; **prices and crowds** are at their lowest between the spring and summer holidays.
- Humidity drives the Japanese habits described elsewhere: **indoor drying**, **dehumidifiers**, **mould prevention** (see those articles).

> **[Verify]** Normals and typical rainy-season dates with the Japan Meteorological Agency.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It pours every day

**About four days in ten** see rain, and much of it is light. Plan **indoor alternatives**, not an indoor holiday.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Bring a heavy raincoat

At **26°C and 80% humidity, a rubberised coat is a sauna**. Locals use **umbrellas** (a ¥500 one from any convenience store) and a **light, breathable shell** at most. A **folding umbrella** in the bag is the universal solution.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Trainers are fine

Wet canvas shoes **do not dry overnight** in this humidity. Bring **shoes that shed water or dry fast**, plus a second pair; hotels rarely have drying facilities.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Laundry will dry in the room

**It will not** — and it will smell. Use a **coin laundry with a dryer** (see that article) or the hotel's laundry service.

## Misunderstanding 5｜It is a bad time to visit

Tsuyu is when **residents do their own travelling**: hydrangea temples (Kamakura's Meigetsu-in), rice paddies just planted, and hotel rates at their lowest. Bring a folding umbrella and quick-dry clothes and the season is fine.
