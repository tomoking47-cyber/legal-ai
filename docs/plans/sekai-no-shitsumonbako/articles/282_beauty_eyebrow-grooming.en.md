# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about eyebrow grooming?｜Japan Questions #282

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/eyebrow-grooming` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Eyebrows set the impression of a face, and the fashionable shape changes with the times. This article is not about fashion. It covers the safety of plucking, shaving and drawing, and the hygiene of the tools — including where Japanese law places eyebrow tattooing.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "the more you pluck, the neater the brow." Repeated plucking weakens the follicle, and hair may stop growing back. The basics are: "pluck only a few stray hairs on the outer edge, and shape with scissors and a razor", "shave with a clean blade after protecting the skin", and "when drawing, add only where hair is missing."

## Methods and points to note

| Method | Suited to | Points to note |
|---|---|---|
| **Plucking (tweezers)** | **The few clearly unwanted hairs** on the outer edge and between the brows | **Repeated plucking can stop regrowth.** Inflamed follicles (folliculitis). **Do not pluck the inner or lower edge** (the shape cannot be restored) |
| **Shaving (eyebrow razor or electric trimmer)** | Fine hairs around the brow | **Clean blade; protect the skin with moisturiser or shaving gel.** **Do not go back and forth over the same spot.** Replace disposable blades after a few uses |
| **Trimming (eyebrow scissors and comb)** | Evening out length | Comb the hairs upward and cut **only what sticks out**. Over-cutting cannot be undone |
| **Drawing (pencil or powder)** | Filling in sparse areas | **Add hair by hair**; do not outline the shape with a line |
| **Eyebrow tint or brow mascara** | Changing colour | **Dermatitis from the dye** in tints has been reported. **Patch test** |
| **Cosmetic tattooing (アートメイク, āto meiku — semi-permanent brow tattooing)** | Long-lasting colour | **A medical procedure** (pigment is placed with a needle). **Unlicensed practice is illegal**; harm to health has been reported |

- Eyebrow hairs have a **short growth phase (about 1–2 months)**, and regrowth after plucking takes **several weeks to several months**. **Repeated irritation can send the follicle into a resting state.**
- **Eyebrow tattooing is a medical act under the Medical Practitioners Act** (the MHLW notice of 2001, restated explicitly in a **2023 notice**; a 2020 Supreme Court decision held that tattooing by a tattooist is not a medical act, but **cosmetic tattooing — drawing brows and eyeliner — kept its status as one**). A **2025 MHLW notice** states that, besides work by unlicensed practitioners, **a procedure carried out by nurses alone without a doctor's examination may also be illegal**. **Having it done at a beauty salon is illegal**, and the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan has received reports of harm to health.

> **[Verify]** Check the legal status of cosmetic tattooing against the MHLW notice (Iseii-hatsu No. 105, 2001) and the published materials of the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Plucking gives the neatest brow

**Pluck only a few hairs on the outer edge.** Shaping with **scissors and a razor** is the method you can undo.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Razors are bad for the skin, so never use one

**With a clean blade and a protective gel, shaving is generally described as less of a strain on skin and follicles than plucking.** **Replace the blade after a few uses** and **dry it after use**.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Pluck under the brow to make the eyes look bigger

**The underside of the brow grows back poorly, and the distance between eye and brow cannot be restored.** Keep the underside to a minimum with **a razor or scissors**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Eyebrow tint is a cosmetic, so it is safe

**The dye can cause dermatitis.** **Patch test 48 hours in advance** and keep it out of the eyes. If **the eyelid swells**, see a dermatology clinic (covered by Japanese health insurance).

## Misunderstanding 5｜Eyebrow tattooing is a quick job at a beauty salon

**Placing pigment in the skin with a needle is a medical act**, and it is lawful **only when a doctor has examined and given instructions and a doctor or nurse performs it at a medical institution.** The MHLW's 2025 notice states that **a procedure carried out by nurses alone, without a doctor's examination, may also be illegal.** **Procedures at beauty salons or home salons are illegal, and infection, allergy and colour change have been reported.**
