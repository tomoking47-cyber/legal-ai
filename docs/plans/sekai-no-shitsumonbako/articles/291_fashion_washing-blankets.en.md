# Q. What do people get wrong about washing blankets?｜Japan Questions #291

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/washing-blankets` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Blankets are often assumed to be "too big to wash", and people in many countries admit to not having washed one for years. In Japan, larger domestic washing machines and the standardised care-label system mean more blankets can now be washed at home. For the rest, the big-drum machines at a coin laundry (コインランドリー) do the job.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "you can't wash a blanket at home". If the care label shows a machine-wash or hand-wash symbol, you can wash it in a machine with enough capacity (as a guide, 7 kg or more for one single blanket). Acrylic and polyester are easy to wash; wool is hand-wash only, depending on the label. The key point is to dry it completely.

## Guide by material

| Material | Washable? | How to wash | Drying | Rough frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Acrylic / polyester (fleece, microfibre)** | **Almost always, subject to the label** | **Fold it accordion-style (屛風畳み) and put it in a mesh laundry bag**, use the machine's **blanket course or gentle cycle**, neutral detergent. **Fabric softener helps against static, but stick to the stated dose** | **Hang it over two laundry poles in an M shape** so air passes through. **Tumble dryer on low if the label allows** | **2–3 times** a season, and always before storing |
| **Cotton blanket** | Yes | As above | As above | As above |
| **Wool / cashmere** | **Only if the label carries a hand-wash symbol** | 30°C (86°F) or below, neutral detergent, press-wash gently | Dry flat, in the shade | Often dry-cleaned at the end of the season |
| **Electric blanket** | **Only those labelled "fully washable" (丸洗い可)** | **Remove the controller (connector)**, follow the label — gentle cycle, mesh bag | **Dry it completely in the shade, and do not switch it on until it is dry**. **Do not tumble-dry it** | — |

- Washing one single blanket (about 1.5–2 kg) needs a machine rated **7–8 kg or more**. Once the load passes **80% of the drum**, it cannot turn and will not wash. The **large machines at a coin laundry** (15–25 kg) are made for blankets and futons.
- A blanket absorbs **night sweat (about 200 mL a night), skin oil and dander**, which **feed dust mites**. **Mites die at 50°C in 20–30 minutes, and faster at 60°C or above**, so a **hot tumble-dry** (if the label allows) is one countermeasure.
- Care labels follow **JIS L0001** (Japan uses the international symbols). A blanket label typically shows **a tub with a number (often 40), a line for gentle action, and whether tumble-drying is allowed**.

> **[Verify]** Check the meaning of the symbols against the Consumer Affairs Agency's "Care labelling" page, and the mite-kill conditions against the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Public Health documents. Capacity figures are from manufacturers' guidance.

## Misunderstanding 1｜You don't need to wash a blanket, just air it

**Sun-drying does not kill mites, and sweat and skin oil stay in the fibre.** Wash it a few times a season, or use **a hot tumble-dry**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Just roll it up and put it in the machine

**Fold it accordion-style (zigzag), put it in a mesh bag, dirty side out.** Rolled up, **water does not reach the middle, and the uneven load strains the machine during spinning.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Acrylic blankets don't need softener

Softener is useful **to reduce static and stiffness**. Keep to the **stated dose**. **Unlike towels, which need to stay absorbent, blankets are a good use for it.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Half a day on the line and it's dry

**It is thick, and the centre dries slowly.** **Hang it in an M shape over two poles, and turn it over part-way.** **Put away damp, it grows mould and smells.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Electric blankets can't be washed

**Products labelled "fully washable" (丸洗い可, maru-arai ka) can be washed.** Detach the controller and connector, then **use a gentle cycle and a mesh bag as the label says.** **Do not wash one without that label** (electric shock, broken wiring).
