# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about using a rice cooker and its keep-warm function?｜Japan Questions #316

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/rice-cooker-tips` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

The rice cooker is the centre of the Japanese kitchen, and "Japanese rice cookers" are popular abroad too. "How many hours can I leave rice on keep-warm?" and "Can I cook other things in it?" are questions the Association for Electric Home Appliances and the makers answer again and again. Here are the numbers behind their answers.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "keep-warm is safe for any number of hours, and the taste does not change." Keep-warm holds the rice at around 70°C, which suppresses bacterial growth, but taste and colour decline within hours, and most makers give 12 hours as the guide (up to 24 on some models). For anything longer, freezing wins on both taste and electricity cost. Cooking other dishes in the cooker is only for compatible models and their listed menus.

## In numbers

| Item | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Keep-warm temperature | **About 70–75°C** (a range that suppresses bacterial growth) | Manufacturer materials |
| Keep-warm guide | **Within 12 hours** (**up to 24 hours** on some models). **Taste declines after 5–6 hours**: yellowing and smell | Major makers' instruction manuals |
| Keep-warm electricity | **About ¥0.4–0.6 per hour** (15–20 W), **about ¥5–7 for 12 hours**. **Freezing and reheating in the microwave usually costs less overall** | Association for Electric Home Appliances; Agency for Natural Resources and Energy |
| Cooking electricity | **About ¥4–6 per batch** (5.5 gō — 合, roughly 1 litre of uncooked rice — about 150 Wh) | Same |
| Lifespan of a rice cooker | **6–10 years** (the inner pot's non-stick coating degrades in 3–5 years) | Association for Electric Home Appliances |

- **Leaving the rice paddle (shamoji) inside, or the lid open, during keep-warm** causes **drying, bacteria and smell**. **Keep the paddle outside.**
- **Cooking other dishes in the rice cooker (cakes, stews, low-temperature cooking)** is **only for compatible models and their listed menus**. NITE (Japan's product-safety agency) has reports of **blocked steam vents, deformed inner pots and boil-overs** causing breakdowns in non-compatible models. **Under-heating in low-temperature cooking (food poisoning)** is another risk.
- **Washing rice in the inner pot** **damages the non-stick coating**, so most makers advise **rinsing the rice in a separate bowl**, or doing it only in models labelled "rice can be washed in the inner pot".

> **[Verify]** Check keep-warm times and electricity costs in your model's instruction manual and on the maker's website, and accident cases with NITE.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Keep-warm is safe for any number of hours

**Around 70°C suppresses bacterial growth**, but **some models switch keep-warm off, or let the temperature drop, after 24 hours**, and **the taste falls within hours**. **Within 12 hours; beyond that, freeze.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Keep-warm costs almost nothing

**¥0.4–0.6 an hour is small, but 12 hours equals one cooking cycle.** **Portion and freeze as soon as it is cooked, then microwave** — **better on both taste and electricity**.

## Misunderstanding 3｜A rice cooker can cook anything

**Only compatible models and their listed menus.** **Thick, sticky dishes (curry, stew, cake) and large amounts of oil or baking soda** cause **blocked steam vents, breakdowns and damage to the inner pot** in non-compatible models.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Washing rice in the inner pot is normal

**It damages the coating, and the rice starts to stick.** **Rinse in a bowl**, or **check for the "washing in the inner pot OK" (内釜洗米OK) label**. **Avoid metal measuring cups and paddles too.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Use the cooker until it dies

**If the inner pot's coating peels, replace just the inner pot** (most makers sell it as a spare part). **Failing to cook, keep-warm not working, or a strange smell** means the body has reached the end of its life. **Spare parts are held for 6 years after production ends** as the industry guide (see our article on warranties and repairs).
