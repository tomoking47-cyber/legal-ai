# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about cosmetic samples and travel sizes?｜Japan Questions #348

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/cosmetic-samples-and-travel-sizes` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Decanting cosmetics into small bottles for a trip, and saving up sample sachets to use later, are both standard practice in Japan. Both raise points about hygiene and about aviation rules. Visitors often ask why Japan's travel-size range is so extensive.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "decanted cosmetics keep the same quality as the original." Decanting lets in air and bacteria, the container material can alter the ingredients, and most makers do not recommend it. For travel, the maker's own travel sizes or single-use sachets are the reliable option. For cabin baggage on international flights, liquids go in containers of 100 mL or less inside a 1 L bag — that is the international rule.

## Points to weigh up

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Decanting** | Most makers **advise against it.** **Air and bacteria get in, container materials (polystyrene etc.) can alter the ingredients, and preservation weakens.** **If you decant, use a clean container and finish it within a few days to a week** |
| **Samples (sachets)** | **Single use.** **Once opened, use it up.** **Even unopened, the thin packaging is vulnerable to light and heat; about 1 year is the guide** |
| **Travel sizes (mini bottles)** | Made by the maker **with the same formula and container as the full size.** **Labelled (ingredients, marketing authorisation holder)** and **hygienic** |
| **Cabin baggage (international flights)** | **Liquids, gels and aerosols in containers of 100 mL or less, in a clear bag of 1 L in total** |
| **Cabin baggage (Japanese domestic flights)** | **No 100 mL limit** (but **aerosols are limited to 0.5 L per container, 2 L in total**) |
| **Aerosols (spray cans)** | **Volume limits apply on aircraft**. **Japan Post (Yu-Pack and the rest) will not accept spray cans that use flammable gas at all** (if no flammable gas is used, they can be sent with that noted in the description of contents). Some courier firms handle them by road only (see the posting article) |

- The **mandatory labelling** of cosmetics (ingredients, marketing authorisation holder, expiry date, etc.) is on **the container or the outer box**, so **decanting loses the label and you may no longer know what is inside** — a problem when you need to identify an allergen.
- Japan's **travel-size market** grew out of **frequent business and domestic travel** and **the sample culture (samples given with purchases)**, and **100-yen shops and drugstores (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.) have dedicated sections.**
- **Sunscreen sprays and antiperspirant sprays** are **aerosols**: **100 mL or less on international flights, 0.5 L or less on domestic flights.**

> **[Verify]** Check the aircraft liquid and aerosol rules against the latest guidance from the Civil Aviation Bureau (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism), and the decanting cautions against Japan Cosmetic Industry Association and maker material.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Decanting is economical and just as hygienic

**Air and bacteria get in and the preservatives are diluted.** Only on the basis of **using it up quickly**, in **a clean container.** **The maker's travel size** is the surer option.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Samples keep for years

**Sachets are thin and degrade with light and heat.** Aim for **within 1 year**, and **use the whole sachet once it is opened.** **Throw away anything separated, discoloured or smelling off.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Domestic flights also have a 100 mL limit

**Japanese domestic flights have no 100 mL limit on liquids** (aerosols are a separate matter). **International flights: 100 mL containers, 1 L bag.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Spray cans are cosmetics, so there are no restrictions

**Aerosols are volume-limited on aircraft, and Japan Post does not accept spray cans containing flammable gas at all.** **Limits apply to checked baggage (the hold) too.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜A 100-yen-shop bottle is good enough for decanting

**Depending on the material (polystyrene etc.), the contents can deform the container or leach substances from it.** Choose bottles marked **"化粧品用" (for cosmetics) or "PP/PE"**, and **do not keep refilling the same bottle with the same product.**
