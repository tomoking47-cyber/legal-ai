# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about tiredness and swelling from standing all day?｜Japan Questions #366

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/standing-all-day` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Many jobs in Japan are done on your feet: retail, kitchens, healthcare, factory lines. Standing work is often assumed to be "healthier than a desk job". But standing for long hours carries its own strain, and it is linked to swollen legs, lower-back pain and varicose veins. Here are the public sources and what you can do.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "standing is always healthier than sitting." Sitting too much is a real problem, but standing still for long periods also leads to pooled blood in the legs (swelling, varicose veins) and strain on the back and feet. The key is "do not hold the same posture". For standing work the measures are: move your feet, shift your weight from side to side, sit or raise your legs during breaks, and get the shoes and floor right.

## Points to weigh

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| The strain of standing work | **Blood pools in the leg veins → swelling and heaviness.** **Over the long term, a risk of varicose veins in the legs.** **Strain on the lower back, the sole of the foot (plantar fascia) and the knees** |
| Guide | **Sit or walk for a few minutes every hour.** **Do not keep standing motionless in the same spot** |
| What you can do on the spot | **Raise and lower your heels (the calf pump)**, **shift your weight from side to side**, **rest one foot on a low step**, **wiggle your toes** |
| Breaks | **Sit with your feet higher than your heart** (on a stool) for a few minutes |
| Shoes | **Cushioning, a stable heel cup and the right width.** **A heel of 3 cm or less** suits long hours. **Insoles** are a supplement (see our article on insoles) |
| Floor | **On hard floors, an anti-fatigue mat** (common at kitchen stations and tills) |
| Compression | **Medical-grade compression stockings** are used to prevent varicose veins and ease symptoms. **Compression socks sold in shops are classed as general goods and cannot carry health claims**; most have weak compression |
| When to see a doctor | **Swelling, pain or warmth in one leg only** (possible blood clot); **bulging veins or skin discolouration** (varicose veins) |

- The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's **Guidelines for Preventing Lower-Back Pain in the Workplace** list, for standing work, **resting one foot on a step, the height of the work surface, cushioned flooring and sitting down at suitable intervals**.
- **Varicose veins in the legs** are attributed to **standing work, pregnancy, ageing and heredity**, and are **more common in women**. **Calf exercise and compression stockings** are the standard advice from vascular surgeons for prevention.
- The Physical Activity Guide 2023 frames the question **not as "which is healthier, sitting or standing work" but as "how often you change posture"**.

> **[Verify]** Check the standing-work section of the lower-back pain guidelines, and the explanation of varicose veins against materials from the Japanese Society of Phlebology and similar bodies.

## Misunderstanding 1｜A standing job means you get enough exercise

**Standing still counts for little as physical activity** (about 1.3–2 METs), and in terms of **pooled blood in the legs** it is a separate problem from sitting. **You need to walk and move.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜My legs are swollen because I drank too much water

**Swelling from standing work is gravity pulling blood and fluid into the legs.** **Restricting fluids backfires** (dehydration thickens the blood). **Work your calves and raise your legs.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Shop-bought compression socks prevent varicose veins

**Shop-bought compression socks are general goods: they cannot carry health claims and their compression is weak.** **Medical-grade compression stockings have their pressure chosen by a doctor or a specialist shop.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜If you are tired, sitting down in your break is enough

**Raising your feet above heart level helps swollen legs more than just sitting.** **Even a few minutes.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Choose shoes by looks; you will get used to them

**Standing for long hours in ill-fitting shoes causes pain in the soles, knees and lower back.** **Cushioning, width and a stable heel.** **Keep heels low.**
