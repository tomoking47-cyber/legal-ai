# Q. What do people get wrong about Shinkansen tickets and rail passes?｜Japan Questions #387

- Shelf: `inbound` ／ URL: `/en/questions/shinkansen-tickets-and-rail-passes` ／ **Caution: prices changed October 2023**
- Status: **Draft — sources unverified**

The Shinkansen is the backbone of a Japan trip and its ticketing is where visitors lose the most money — either by buying a rail pass that no longer pays off, or by not knowing that a ticket is two tickets and a seat is three kinds.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that the Japan Rail Pass is automatically the best deal. Since the October 2023 price rise (about 70%), the 7-day pass at ¥50,000 only pays off for roughly three or more long Shinkansen trips; a Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka itinerary is cheaper with individual tickets or a regional pass. A Shinkansen ticket is a base fare plus a limited-express surcharge, sold as one or two pieces, with unreserved, reserved and Green (first-class) seats.

| Break-even | The 7-day pass roughly equals **Tokyo–Kyoto–Hiroshima–Tokyo**; below that, buy tickets |

## Tickets and seats

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Base fare (乗車券)** | Distance-based; valid several days |
| **Limited express ticket (特急券)** | The Shinkansen surcharge; specifies the train and seat class |
| **Unreserved (自由席)** | Cars 1–3 (or 1–5) on most trains; first come, first served; **¥500–900 cheaper**; you may have to stand at peak |
| **Reserved (指定席)** | Assigned seat; **book online (Smart EX, JR East eki-net) or at the machine** |
| **Green car (グリーン車)** | First class, wider seats, **about +¥5,000** |
| **Gran Class** | Premium on Tohoku/Hokuriku lines |
| **Oversized-baggage seat** | Free reservation for bags 160–250 cm (see the luggage article) |

- **Smart EX** (Tokaido/Sanyo/Kyushu) and **eki-net** (JR East) sell tickets online in English with **IC-card or QR boarding**; discounts of ¥200–1,000 for booking ahead.
- **Peak periods** (Golden Week, Obon, New Year — see that article) fill reserved seats **a month ahead**; unreserved cars can be standing-only.

> **[Verify]** Fares and pass prices with JR Group's Japan Rail Pass site and JR Central; regional pass prices change yearly.

## Misunderstanding 1｜The JR Pass always saves money

**Since October 2023 it rarely does for a standard Tokyo–Kansai trip.** Price out your actual legs; **regional passes** cover most itineraries for less.

## Misunderstanding 2｜One ticket, one price

A Shinkansen journey is **base fare + limited-express surcharge**, sometimes printed as **two tickets** — insert **both** into the gate together. Online tickets on an IC card avoid this.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Unreserved means no seat exists

**Unreserved cars have seats**; you just are not assigned one. Off-peak, you will sit. **Peak periods and Friday evenings**, reserve.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Buy at the counter

**Ticket machines have English**, and online booking (Smart EX, eki-net) is faster with QR or IC boarding. Counters are for passes and complex routes.

## Misunderstanding 5｜The Nozomi is off-limits with a pass

Since 2023 the **Nozomi and Mizuho are usable with the JR Pass for a supplement**; the Hikari takes only about 30 minutes longer to Kyoto and is included free.
