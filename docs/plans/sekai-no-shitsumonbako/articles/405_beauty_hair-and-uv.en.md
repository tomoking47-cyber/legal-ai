# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about protecting hair from UV?｜Japan Questions #405

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/hair-and-uv` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Sun protection for the skin is now routine, but hair and scalp are easily forgotten. In Japanese summers you will see hair UV sprays on every drugstore shelf (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.), which raises the questions "Does UV really damage hair?" and "Do I need a sunscreen for my hair?" Here we look at how hair behaves and at the approach taken by the Ministry of the Environment's UV manual.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "hair is dead cells, so UV does not matter." Hair does not regenerate, but UV damages the cuticle, breaks down the pigment so the colour fades, and leaves the hair dry and frizzy. The scalp is skin and can burn. The basic protection is a hat and a parasol; hair UV spray is a supplement.

## Points to consider

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effect on hair | **UV damages the protein of the cuticle**; **melanin breaks down and the colour fades (turns reddish-brown)**; **dyed colour fades**; **dryness and breakage** |
| Effect on scalp | **Sunburn (inflammation), as on any skin**. **The parting and the crown** burn most easily. Some explanations describe **scalp sunburn as one cause of hair loss** |
| Most reliable protection | **A hat (brim 7 cm or wider) and a parasol** (Ministry of the Environment manual). Choose **UV-blocking hats** by their shading rate |
| Hair UV spray | A **cosmetic** ("protects the hair", etc.). **SPF/PA ratings are standards for skin**; **there is no measurement standard for protection of hair**. **A supplement** |
| Skin sunscreen on the scalp | Some people **apply it along the parting**, but it is **greasy and hard to wash out**. **Scalp-specific spray types** exist |
| Sea and pool | **Chlorine and salt plus UV speed up fading and dryness**. **Wet hair is more vulnerable to UV**. **Hat, and rinse with fresh water** |
| Washing | **On days of UV exposure, moisturise thoroughly (treatment)** |

- The Ministry of the Environment's manual names **"hat, parasol, clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses"** as the basics of UV protection, and lists hats and parasols as the means of protecting **the head and eyes**. **It says little about the hair itself**.
- **Fading of hair colour** is mainly caused by **UV and washing (shampoo)**. **Summer colour fades faster** is the usual explanation in the hair trade.
- **Hair UV spray** is **a standard Japanese summer product**, but use it as a supplement in the knowledge that **an SPF figure is "a measurement on skin"** and **not a figure for protection of hair**.

> **[Verify]** Check the relevant passages of the Ministry of the Environment manual, and the effects of UV on hair against standard hair-science texts.

## Misunderstanding 1｜UV does not damage hair

**Cuticle damage, fading and dryness** all happen. **Because hair does not regenerate, prevention is what counts**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜SPF50 hair spray protects hair 50 times over

**SPF is a measurement standard for skin**, **not a figure for protection of hair**. **A hat and parasol are reliable**; spray is a supplement.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Black hair is resistant to UV

**With more melanin it is "slower to change colour", as with skin tanning, but damage still occurs**. **Dyed hair fades faster**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜The hair protects the scalp

**The parting, the crown and thinning areas do burn**. **A hat**, or **a scalp spray**. **Redness, itching and peeling** are sunburn symptoms.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Getting wet in the sea protects against UV

**Wet hair lets UV through more easily, and salt or chlorine on top of that is the most damaging combination**. **A hat, and rinse with fresh water**.
