# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about home beauty devices and electric cleansing brushes?｜Japan Questions #407

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/beauty-devices-and-cleansing-brushes` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Home beauty devices (ion, EMS, LED, ultrasound, RF and so on) and electric cleansing brushes are a growing market in Japan and popular with visitors, who often take one home. The questions are "Is it a medical device or just a gadget?" and "How much can it actually claim to do?" Here we sort them by their legal category in Japan and by the accidents reported to consumer bodies.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "a beauty device gives the same results as a salon or a clinic." Most home beauty devices are either general goods or general (Class I, low-risk) medical devices, and all they may claim is along the lines of "cleans the skin", "helps cosmetics spread" or "massage". "Improves sagging" and "erases wrinkles" are not permitted claims, and the devices differ from clinic equipment in both power and purpose. Burns and skin irritation have been reported, so keeping to the time and body-area limits in the manual is the basic condition of use.

## Categories and accidents

| Category | Examples | What it may claim | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General goods (electrical appliance)** | Most beauty devices (EMS, ion, LED, hot/cold), electric cleansing brushes | **"Cleans the skin", "helps cosmetics spread", "massage"** and the like. **No efficacy claims (improvement, healing)** | **PSE mark** (Japan's electrical-safety mark). **Follow the manual's usage time, frequency and prohibited areas** |
| **General medical device (Class I)** | Some home massagers | **Within the notified scope, e.g. "loosens muscle stiffness"** | A notification number is shown |
| **Controlled medical device (Class II)** | Some home light-therapy and low-frequency devices | Approved indications | A certification number |
| **Clinic equipment** | Medical RF, laser, HIFU | At a doctor's discretion | **Private cosmetic medicine (自由診療, not covered by health insurance)** |

- **The Consumer Affairs Agency and the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan** receive complaints about **burns from home beauty devices (RF, heat), rashes, eye pain (LED) and skin damage from EMS stimulation**. Most are caused by **exceeding the usage time, working the same spot repeatedly, or using a prohibited area (around the eyes, mucous membranes)**.
- **Electric cleansing brushes** attract complaints of **irritation and redness from friction**; many makers advise **against daily use** (once or twice a week, a soft brush, no pressing). **Not suited to sensitive or acne-prone skin**.
- **Claims for a "device plus dedicated cosmetic"** cannot exceed **the 56 permitted cosmetic claims** (the list set by Japan's health ministry). **"Penetration" means only as far as the stratum corneum** (the outermost layer of skin).

> **[Verify]** Check the category descriptions against the PMDA medical-device database, and the accident cases against Consumer Affairs Agency and National Consumer Affairs Center materials.

## Misunderstanding 1｜A beauty device firms the skin and erases wrinkles

**General goods and Class I medical devices may not claim this**. **Massage, cleansing and helping cosmetics spread** are the limits. **Clinic equipment is a different thing**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Longer use means more effect

**The main cause of burns and irritation is exceeding the usage time**. Keep to **the manual's time (usually a few minutes per area, once a day, a few times a week)**.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Daily use of a cleansing brush means cleaner pores

**Daily friction causes irritation and redness**. **Once or twice a week, a soft brush, no pressing**. **Not for sensitive or acne-prone skin**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜It can be used anywhere on the body

Most products **prohibit use around the eyes, on mucous membranes, wounds, moles and inflamed areas**. **Do not hold RF or heat devices on one spot for long**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜An expensive device equals a salon

**Home devices are designed with reduced power**, and **may not claim results equal to a salon or a clinic**. **Price and effect are separate things**. Stay within **what you can keep up**.
