# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about choosing a washing machine (top-loader or front-loading drum)?｜Japan Questions #414

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/choosing-a-washing-machine` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

In Japan, top-loading machines (縦型, tategata) are the norm and front-loading drum machines (ドラム式, doramu-shiki) are gaining ground — the reverse of many other countries, where drums are standard. Which gets clothes cleaner, what they cost in electricity and water, and how they dry: we set it out from materials by the Association for Electric Home Appliances and the Agency for Natural Resources and Energy.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "drum machines are newer, so they are better at everything." A top-loader rubs clothes together in plenty of water, so it is strong on mud and sebum, and the machine itself is cheaper. A drum machine lifts and drops clothes in little water, so it saves water, causes less friction, and is good at drying (heat pump) with lower electricity costs. Choose by your household's conditions: whether you will use the dryer, the space you have, and the kind of dirt you deal with.

## Comparison (a general summary based on AEHA and makers' materials)

| Item | Top-loader | Front-loading drum |
|---|---|---|
| Washing action | **Plenty of water; clothes rub against each other (kneading wash)** | **Little water; clothes are lifted and dropped (tumble wash)** |
| Cleaning | **Strong on mud, sebum and ground-in soil** | Generally described as **strong on sebum, but slightly weaker on mud and solid dirt** |
| Wear on clothes | **More friction** (pilling, colour loss → see the laundry-net article) | **Less friction** |
| Water use | **About 100–150 L per cycle** | **About 60–90 L per cycle** (without drying) |
| Drying | **Mostly heater type, high electricity cost** (about ¥30–50 per cycle), creases easily | **Mostly heat-pump type, low electricity cost** (about ¥15–30 per cycle); **if you will use the dryer every day, choose a drum** |
| Price of the machine | **Cheaper** (¥50,000–150,000) | **Dearer** (¥150,000–300,000) |
| Installation | **Shallow, about 60 cm wide**. **Loaded from the top** | **Deeper and wider**; check the **space for the door to swing** and the **size of the drip tray (防水パン, bōsui pan — the plastic tray under the machine in most Japanese homes)**. **Loaded from the front (easier on the back)** |
| Cleaning the machine | Tub-clean cycle (see the article on the washing tub) | **Mould behind the rubber door seal**, filters |
| Share in Japan | **About 70%** (2020s) | **About 30% and rising** |

- The **Agency for Natural Resources and Energy's "Energy-saving performance catalogue"** lets you **compare annual electricity and water use model by model**. **Whether you use the dryer** makes the biggest difference to electricity costs.
- **Heat-pump drying (drum)** works at a **low temperature of about 60°C** and is gentle on clothes; **heater drying (common in top-loaders)** runs hot and shrinks clothes more easily (see the dryer article).
- **Capacity**: reckon on **about 1.5 kg per person per day**. **7–8 kg for a family of four**, **7 kg or more if you want to wash blankets** (see the blanket article).

## Misunderstanding 1｜A drum machine is better at everything

**On cleaning (mud, solid dirt) and price the top-loader wins; on water saving, drying and gentleness to clothes the drum wins**. **Choose by your conditions**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜A drum saves water, so the water bill drops a lot

**The difference is a few tens of litres per cycle**, which comes to **a few thousand yen a year**. **Recovering the price difference (¥100,000 or more) takes a long time**. **The electricity for drying** makes the bigger difference.

## Misunderstanding 3｜The dryer function is the same on both

**Heat pump (drum) is low heat, low power; heater type (top-loader) is high heat, high power**. **If you will dry every day, choose a drum** is the usual conclusion.

## Misunderstanding 4｜The bigger the capacity, the better

**Running small loads in a big machine wastes water and electricity**. **Number in the household × 1.5 kg, plus a margin**. **7 kg or more if you want to wash blankets and duvets**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Leave installation to the delivery crew and it will fit

Measure beforehand: **the drip tray, the tap height, the delivery route (stairs, corridor width) and which way the door opens**. **Drum machines that could not be brought in and were returned** are a common story.
