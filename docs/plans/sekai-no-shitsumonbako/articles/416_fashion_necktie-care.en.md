# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about looking after a necktie?｜Japan Questions #416

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/necktie-care` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

A tie is worn day after day but almost never washed, so people ask "can it be washed at all?", "will the creases come out?" and "does the knot mark go away?". In Japan the summer "Cool Biz" dress code has cut down how often ties are worn, yet they remain compulsory at weddings, funerals and formal ceremonies, and the know-how for caring for them has survived.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "a tie cannot be washed, so once it is dirty you replace it." Most ties (silk) cannot be washed at home, but daily care — untie it as soon as you take it off, rest it hanging or loosely rolled, steam out the creases — makes it last for years, and stains can be dealt with by a dry cleaner. A polyester tie whose care label shows the hand-wash symbol can be washed at home.

## The basics of care

| Situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Taking it off** | **Do not pull the knot to slide it out; undo it by reversing the steps you tied it in** | Pulling stretches the fabric and the interlining |
| **Afterwards** | **Hang it on a hanger overnight (or lay it loosely rolled)** | The weight lets the knot creases fall out |
| **Rotation** | **Do not wear the same tie on consecutive days** | Silk needs time to recover |
| **Creases and knot marks** | **Hang it in the steam of the bathroom, or hold a garment steamer just off the fabric**. **Never iron it directly (pressing cloth, low heat, iron held above the fabric)** | Silk goes shiny and the interlining is flattened |
| **Stains** | **Press at once with a dry cloth (do not rub)**. **Water causes water rings**. **Tell the dry cleaner what the stain is** | Silk is sensitive to water |
| **Washing** | **Most silk ties cannot be washed at home**. **A polyester tie with the hand-wash symbol on its label: press-wash in neutral detergent (中性洗剤, chūsei senzai), reshape and dry in the shade** | Follow the label |
| **Storage** | **Hang (tie hanger) or roll into a compartment**. **Use a moth repellent (moths eat silk)**, **keep out of direct sunlight** | — |
| **Dry cleaning** | **Once a season, or when soiled**. **Say "it is a necktie" so the press does not flatten the interlining** | — |

- Ties are **covered by the Household Goods Quality Labelling Act (Textile Goods Quality Labelling Regulations)**, so **the fibre composition (silk, polyester, etc.) has to be labelled**. **A care label (Japan's international JIS L0001 symbols) is not required on ties, however, and some carry none**. **The shell, the lining and the interlining** may be different materials.
- **Knot marks** come out with **steam** in most cases, because **silk fibres recover with humidity and warmth**.
- **Since Cool Biz came in, ties are worn less often**. **From FY2021 the Ministry of the Environment dropped the nationwide period (previously May to September) and now asks each person to judge by the temperature and their workplace.** Ties remain compulsory at **weddings, funerals, interviews and ceremonies**, and it is customary to keep **a plain black tie (funerals) and a white tie (celebrations)** separately.

> **[Verify]** The scope of the labelling duty is based on Consumer Affairs Agency materials; the care advice on general guidance from the necktie and textile industries.

## Misunderstanding 1｜A tie cannot be washed, so a stain is the end of it

**A dry cleaner can deal with stains**, and **daily care prevents most soiling**. **Polyester ties can be washed at home if the label allows it**.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Sliding the knot out is the quick way to take it off

**It stretches the fabric and the interlining, and the knot marks stop coming out**. **Untie it**.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Iron the creases out

**An iron placed directly on silk makes it shiny and flattens the interlining**. **Use bathroom steam or a steamer held off the fabric**; if you must iron, **pressing cloth, low heat, iron held above**.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Dab a stain with water

**Water leaves rings on silk**. **Press with a dry cloth and take it to the dry cleaner**.

## Misunderstanding 5｜When not in use it can be kept anywhere

**Moths eat silk and sunlight fades it**. **Store with a moth repellent, in the dark, hung or rolled**.
