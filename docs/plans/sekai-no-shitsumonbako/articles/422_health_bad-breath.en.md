# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about bad breath?｜Japan Questions #422

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/bad-breath` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Bad breath is hard to notice in yourself and awkward for others to mention. Since 2020, posts saying "I noticed my own breath behind a mask" have increased on forums abroad too. Here we look at why most causes lie in the mouth, and at the problem of worrying too much.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "bad breath comes from the stomach." About 90% of bad breath starts in the mouth (tongue coating, gum disease, reduced saliva); the stomach is rarely the cause. The basics are cleaning the tongue, cleaning between the teeth, water and saliva, and a gum-disease check at the dentist. There is also a known condition, halitophobia (自臭症, jishūshō), in which a person worries intensely about bad breath they do not have.

## Causes and what to do

| Cause | Share / features | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Tongue coating (the white film on the tongue)** | **The main source of bad breath.** Bacteria break down food debris and protein into sulphur compounds | **Once a day, gently, with a tongue brush (舌ブラシ, shita-burashi, sold at drugstores), from back to front** (over-scrubbing damages taste and the lining) |
| **Gum disease (periodontitis)** | **Signs in about half of adults.** Inflamed gums and bacteria in periodontal pockets | **Clean between the teeth (floss, interdental brush); cleaning and care at a dental clinic** |
| **Reduced saliva (dry mouth)** | Saliva falls **on waking, when hungry, when nervous, with age and as a side effect of medicines**, and bacteria multiply | **Water, chewing well at meals, chewing gum, breathing through the nose** |
| **Food, drink and tobacco** | Garlic, spring onion, alcohol, cigarettes | Temporary. Reduced by **water and brushing** |
| **Nose, throat and general illness** | Sinusitis, tonsils, diabetes, kidney or liver disease (**rare**) | See a doctor |
| **Stomach** | **Rare** (gas does not normally rise from the oesophagus into the mouth) | — |
| **Halitophobia** | **Worrying intensely about bad breath that is not there** | Have **an instrument-based breath test** at a dental clinic for an objective check |

- The Japan Dental Association explains that **about 90% of bad breath comes from the mouth**, with **tongue coating and gum disease** as the main causes. **"It comes from the stomach" is repeatedly pointed out as a misunderstanding.**
- **Morning breath (physiological bad breath)** happens to everyone and is caused by **reduced saliva during sleep**. **Brushing and a drink of water in the morning** deal with it.
- More people noticed their own breath behind a **mask** because **mouth breathing dries the mouth** and **odour builds up inside the mask**. In most cases **the bad breath itself did not increase**.
- Some dental clinics have **breath-measuring instruments** (Halimeter etc.), which give **an objective reading**. **Mouthwash mainly masks the smell for a while**; **removing the cause (tongue coating, gum disease) comes first**.

> **[Verify]** Check the share of mouth-derived cases and the explanation of causes against materials from the Japan Dental Association and the Japanese Society for Breath Odor Research [日本口臭学会].

## Misunderstanding 1｜Bad breath comes from the stomach

**About 90% is in the mouth.** **Tongue coating and gum disease.** **The stomach is rarely the cause.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Brushing your teeth gets rid of bad breath

**A toothbrush does not remove tongue coating or debris between the teeth.** **Tongue brush once a day; floss or an interdental brush.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Mouthwash solves it

**It masks the smell for a while.** **Remove the cause first.** **Alcohol-based mouthwash dries the mouth and can make things worse.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Scrub the tongue hard and remove all the white

**Over-scrubbing damages taste and the lining.** **Gently, back to front, a few strokes, once a day.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜If I think I smell, I definitely do

**Halitophobia (worrying intensely about bad breath you do not have) is a known condition.** **Get an objective breath test at a dental clinic.** **If there is gum disease, have it seen to; if not, that is reassurance.**
