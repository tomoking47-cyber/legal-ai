# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about cleaning your ears?｜Japan Questions #424

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/ear-cleaning` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

In Japan, ear cleaning is an established habit, and the bamboo ear pick (耳かき, mimikaki) is an everyday household item. Ear, nose and throat (ENT) doctors, however, keep repeating that ear cleaning is basically unnecessary. Here is how earwax works, and what goes wrong (outer-ear infection, perforated eardrum).

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "earwax is dirt, so it should be removed regularly." Earwax is a protective film made of skin and secretions, and jaw movement carries it outwards naturally. The Oto-Rhino-Laryngological Society of Japan (the Japanese ENT society) advises: cleaning is basically unnecessary; if you do it, once or twice a month and no deeper than about 1 cm from the opening. Pushing wax deeper with a cotton bud, and scratching the ear canal with an ear pick, are the most common problems.

## Points to weigh

| Item | Detail | Source |

| When to see a doctor | **Muffled hearing, a blocked feeling, pain, itching that continues, discharge** | — |

- **The Consumer Affairs Agency** warns that **children's eardrums are being perforated during ear cleaning (the child moved, was bumped, or a sibling poked)**. **Children move, so take them to an ENT clinic (耳鼻科, jibika).**
- **Ear-pick culture**: the bamboo mimikaki with its fluffy bonten tuft is a Japanese household item, but **ENT doctors advise "do not touch a place you cannot see with a hard tool."** **The skin of the ear canal is thin, and infection through a scratch (otitis externa)** is common.
- **People who wear earphones or hearing aids for long hours** are **more likely to push wax in, and humidity can lead to otitis externa**. **Keep them clean and take them out now and then.**

> **[Verify]** Check the society's advice (frequency, depth), the dry/wet proportions and the accident cases against materials from the Oto-Rhino-Laryngological Society of Japan and the Consumer Affairs Agency.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Earwax is dirt, so remove it every day

**It is a protective film and clears naturally.** **Basically unnecessary; if at all, once or twice a month.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Cleaning deep with a cotton bud gets the ear clean

**Cotton buds push wax deeper and cause blockages.** **No deeper than 1 cm from the opening.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜If the ear pick feels good, it must be right

**The skin of the canal is thin, and scratches lead to inflammation.** **That "got some" feeling can be you scraping skin.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Cleaning a child's ears is a parent's job

**Children move, and eardrums get perforated.** **Take children to an ENT clinic** (health insurance, a few minutes).

## Misunderstanding 5｜If the ear feels blocked, dig it out yourself

**Trying to remove impacted wax yourself pushes it deeper.** **Have it removed at an ENT clinic (insured).** **If muffled hearing continues, see a doctor.**
