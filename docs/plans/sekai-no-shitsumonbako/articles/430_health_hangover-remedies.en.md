# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about dealing with a hangover?｜Japan Questions #430

- Shelf: `health` ／ URL: `/en/health/hangover-remedies` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Every country has its "miracle hangover fix". In Japan the classics are shijimi (freshwater clam) miso soup, umeboshi (salted pickled plum) and ukon (turmeric) drinks. Here is what is solid and what has no evidence, based on the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) drinking guidelines of 2024 and standard physiology.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "there is a food or drink that works on a hangover." The main causes of a hangover are dehydration, low blood sugar, acetaldehyde and poor-quality sleep; what reliably helps is time, fluids, sugar and rest. Commercial drinks and supplements may not claim to prevent or relieve hangovers, and "hair of the dog" (迎え酒, mukae-zake) only postpones the symptoms. Prevention is how much you drink, and water while you drink.

## How a hangover works and what to do

| Cause | Symptoms | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Dehydration** | Headache, thirst, fatigue (alcohol is a diuretic) | **Water, oral rehydration solution or a sports drink, in small sips** (see our article on oral rehydration solutions) |
| **Low blood sugar** | Unsteadiness, cold sweat, weakness | **Sugar (fruit, rice porridge, honey)** |
| **Acetaldehyde** | Nausea, palpitations, facial flushing. **How well you break it down is genetic** (about 40% of Japanese people have a weak enzyme) | **Time** (wait for it to break down). **Hair of the dog slows the breakdown further** |
| **Stomach irritation** | Stomach pain, nausea | **Small amounts of easily digested food; OTC stomach medicine as directed** |
| **Poor sleep quality** | Drowsiness, poor concentration | **Rest** (see our article on alcohol and sleep) |
| **Acetone and inflammatory substances** | General fatigue | Time |

| Popular "fix" | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Shijimi (freshwater clam) miso soup** | **Reasonable as fluid, salt and amino acids.** **A food may not claim "ornithine helps the liver", and no evidence has been confirmed that it eases a hangover** |
| **Ukon (turmeric, curcumin) drinks and supplements** | **Insufficient evidence that they prevent or improve hangovers** (National Institute of Health and Nutrition). **Reports of liver damage** exist; **people with liver disease should take care** |
| **Hair of the dog (mukae-zake)** | **Masks the symptoms for a while and slows the breakdown.** **Risk of alcohol dependence** |
| **Sweating it out in a sauna or with exercise** | **Worsens dehydration; dangerous** (see our sauna article) |
| **Coffee** | **Caffeine wakes you for a while; the diuretic effect worsens dehydration.** A small amount is fine |
| **OTC headache tablets** | **Paracetamol (acetaminophen) taken with alcohol burdens the liver.** **Follow the directions and avoid it straight after drinking** |

- The MHLW's *Guidelines on Health-Conscious Drinking* (2024) recommend **knowing your intake in grams of pure alcohol** and **"food and water before and while drinking"**, and describe a hangover as **"a sign you drank too much"**.
- **About 40% of Japanese people have a weak form of the acetaldehyde-breaking enzyme (ALDH2)** and flush easily. **People with this trait are generally described as getting a hangover from small amounts and as having a higher risk of oesophageal cancer and other conditions.**
- Claims such as **"works on hangovers" or "protects the liver"** are **not permitted for ordinary foods and supplements under Japan's Health Promotion Act and the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations**. **Even Foods with Function Claims (a Japanese labelling category) do not claim "hangover" directly.**

> **[Verify]** Check the 2024 drinking guidelines, the assessment of turmeric in the National Institute of Health and Nutrition's "health foods" database, and the ALDH2 proportion on e-Health Net.

## Misunderstanding 1｜There is a drink that works on hangovers

**No product may make that claim, and no evidence has been confirmed.** **Fluids, sugar, time.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Hair of the dog makes you feel better

**It slows the breakdown and postpones the symptoms.** It is generally described as **a gateway to dependence**.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Sweat the alcohol out

**Almost no alcohol leaves through sweat; dehydration worsens and it is dangerous.** **No sauna, no hard exercise.**

## Misunderstanding 4｜Take ukon beforehand and you're protected

**Insufficient evidence, and there are reports of liver damage.** **Prevention is how much you drink, plus water and food while drinking.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜Take a headache tablet and have another

**Paracetamol with alcohol burdens the liver.** **Avoid painkillers straight after drinking; the next morning, as directed.** **Severe headache, vomiting that will not stop, or confusion** is an emergency (call 119 for an ambulance in Japan): possible acute alcohol poisoning.
