# Q. What are the common misunderstandings about canned and retort-pouch foods, their dates and what to do after opening?｜Japan Questions #434

- Shelf: `trend` ／ URL: `/en/trend/canned-and-retort-foods-after-opening` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Canned food and retort pouches (レトルト食品, retoruto shokuhin — ready meals such as curry sealed in a foil pouch and pressure-sterilised) are the core of Japanese emergency stockpiling, known as "rolling stock" (ローリングストック: eat from your stock and replace it). People ask whether they can eat them past the date, and whether an opened can may go in the fridge as it is. Here we explain how they are made and how the date labels work.

## A. The biggest misunderstanding is "canned and retort foods keep for a long time even after opening." The long life comes from the sealed, heat-sterilised, unopened state; the moment you open it, it is ordinary food. After opening, transfer to another container, refrigerate and finish within 1–2 days. The unopened best-before date is a quality guide, and if a can has no bulging, dents, rust or leaks, it is generally described as still edible after the date.

## What to base your judgement on

| Item | Cans | Retort pouches |
|---|---|---|
| How they are made | **Sealed, then sterilised under pressure and heat (equivalent to 120°C for 4 minutes or more)** | **The same (sealed in a pouch and pressure-heated)** |
| Unopened best-before date | **About 3 years** (varies by product). **It is a "best-before" (賞味期限) quality date, not a safety limit** | **1–2 years** |
| Past the date | **If the can shows no fault (bulging, dents, rust, leaking liquid), it is often still edible.** **Taste and colour decline** | **Same if the pouch has no bulging or tears.** **Flavour fades sooner as oils oxidise** |
| **After opening** | **Transfer from the can to another container (plastic or glass), refrigerate, 1–2 days** | **Take out of the pouch, transfer to a container, refrigerate, 1–2 days** |
| Refrigerating in the can | **No**: once opened, the inner coating is exposed to air, giving **a metallic smell, discolouration and metal leaching** | — |
| Heating | **Never heat the can on a flame or in the microwave** (bursting, sparks). **Empty it out and heat** | **Can be heated in the pouch in hot water (as labelled)**; **microwave only if the pouch says so** |
| Bulging can or pouch | **Never eat it** (botulism and other poisoning from sealed foods) | Same |

- **A can's "3-year best-before date" is the period tested for keeping quality after manufacture**, and **differs by contents and can type (acidic fruit is shorter).** **It does not mean the maker vouches for safety after the date, but a sealed, sterilised can in good condition is generally described as stable for a long time** (Japan Canners Association).
- ***Clostridium botulinum*** **grows in sealed, oxygen-free foods and can make a can bulge.** Never eat anything that **bulges, smells off or spurts on opening.** **Home-made cans and preserving jars need special care.**
- **Disaster stockpiling**: **cans and retort pouches store at room temperature and mostly need no heating, so they are the basis of a stockpile** (rolling stock). **Ring-pull cans that need no opener** are the practical choice.

> **[Verify]** Check the reasoning on best-before dates and handling after opening with the Japan Canners Association and the Consumer Affairs Agency, and the botulism warning with the Food Safety Commission.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Once opened, it keeps if left in the can

**The moment it is opened, it is ordinary food.** **Move it to another container, refrigerate, 1–2 days.**

## Misunderstanding 2｜Throw out any can past its best-before date

**If the can shows no fault, it is often still edible** (quality declines). Dispose of it if there is **bulging, denting, rust or leakage.**

## Misunderstanding 3｜Heat it in the can

**On a flame or in the microwave it can burst or spark.** **Empty it out and heat.** **Some products may be warmed in the can in hot water** (check the label).

## Misunderstanding 4｜Microwave the retort pouch as it is

**Only products labelled microwave-safe.** **Pouches with an aluminium layer spark.** **Hot water is the standard method.**

## Misunderstanding 5｜A bulging can is fine once the gas escapes

**Never eat it.** It may contain **botulinum or other toxins.** **Spurting and off smells are also warning signs.**
