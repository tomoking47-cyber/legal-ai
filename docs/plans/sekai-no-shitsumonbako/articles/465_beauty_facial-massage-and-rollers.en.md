# Q. Do facial massage and face rollers help with sagging and puffiness?｜Japan Questions #465

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/facial-massage-and-rollers` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Facial massage and face rollers (biganrōrā) sold in Japan often promise a "smaller face" (kogao) or a "lift". Many people have felt puffiness go down and their face look fresher for a while. Whether sagging can be "lifted" is a separate question. Starting from how the skin is built, this article separates what these can and cannot do.

## A. Puffiness (excess fluid) is moved along for a while by light massage, and the face looks fresher for a few hours. Sagging, on the other hand, is caused by changes in the collagen, fat and muscle that support the skin, and cannot be pushed back up from outside. If anything, strong friction causes dark pigmentation and fine lines. If you do massage, use plenty of cream or milk lotion so your fingers glide, press so lightly that the skin does not move, and stop after 2–3 minutes per session. Face rollers are sold as "general goods" or "beauty appliances", not medical devices, so there are limits on the effects they may claim.

## What it can and cannot do

| Concern | Cause | With massage or a roller | Realistic measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Puffiness** | Fluid retention from salt, alcohol, lack of sleep and so on | **Eased for a while (a few hours)** | Cut back on salt and alcohol; raise the pillow slightly |
| **Sagging** | Loss of collagen in the dermis, descending fat, changes in the facial muscles | **Does not lift** | Sun protection, moisturising, and a dermatology or cosmetic dermatology clinic if needed |
| **Dullness** | Reduced blood flow, a disturbed outer skin layer, pigmentation | Blood flow gives a brief brightness | Sleep, rethinking how you wash, sun protection |
| **Smaller face** | Bone structure and the amount of fat | **Bone structure does not change.** The face looks slimmer only by the amount of puffiness lost | — |

- **Facial skin is about 1–2 mm thick, and the eyelids about 0.5 mm** — among the thinnest skin on the body. Pressing hard or rubbing easily causes **dark pigmentation from friction (friction melanosis)** and **redness**.
- **If you do it**: make sure your fingers glide with cream, milk lotion or oil; use only enough pressure that the **skin does not bunch up**; move **from the centre outwards and from the bottom upwards**; and keep to **2–3 minutes per session**. Around the eyes, only touch lightly with the pads of the fingers.
- **Legal position of face rollers**: most are "general goods" (zakka) or "beauty appliances", and **not medical devices**. Claims about effects on the body's structure or function, such as "improves sagging" or "lifts", can be a problem under the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act (Japan's cosmetics and drugs law) and the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations (Japan's advertising law).
- **The National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan** has received complaints of **redness, bruising and rashes** caused by facial devices and rollers. Any pressure that hurts is counterproductive.

## Misunderstanding 1｜If I roll every day the sagging will lift

**Sagging is caused by changes inside the skin's structure and does not come back by rolling from outside.** Puffiness going down and the face looking fresher for a while is a different thing from improving sagging.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Pressing harder works better

**The skin is thin, and strong friction causes pigmentation, fine lines and bruising.** "It hurts, so it must be working" does not apply to the face.

## Misunderstanding 3｜I can use the roller directly on bare skin

**Without something to glide on, the friction goes straight into the skin.** Apply milk lotion or cream first, or use it when the skin is wet, such as in the bath.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Draining the lymph flushes out waste and makes the skin beautiful

**Facial puffiness can ease for a while**, but there is no firm evidence that "flushing out waste changes skin quality". This is an area where wording needs care.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Face rollers are medical devices, so they work

**Most are general goods or beauty appliances.** Products approved or certified as medical devices carry a "medical device certification number" (医療機器認証番号) on the label. The most reliable way to judge the claims is to look for that label.
