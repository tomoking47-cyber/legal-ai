# Q. Can I wash a backpack or tote bag? How to do it at home, and what to watch for｜Japan Questions #475

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/backpack-washing` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

The backpack you carry to work or school every day, the tote you take shopping: both are grubby with sweat and hand grease, yet most people keep using them because they do not know how to wash them. Whether a bag can be washed depends on its material and its fittings. This article also covers the randoseru (ランドセル), the stiff leather school satchel Japanese children carry for six years.

## A. Nylon, polyester and cotton bags can be washed at home if the care label (Japan uses the international JIS L0001 symbols) shows a wash symbol. But washing can distort the shape, scratch the metal fittings and weaken any water-repellent finish, so the basic method is: hand-wash in lukewarm water with a neutral detergent, press rather than rub, and dry in the shade for two days until fully dry. Bags with leather handles, a base board or synthetic leather (polyurethane) deteriorate when washed in water, so limit yourself to wiping. A randoseru must never be washed in water.

## Judging by material and parts

| Material or part | Wash in water | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Nylon or polyester (label allows washing)** | **Yes** (hand-wash recommended) | Lukewarm water (**30°C / 86°F or below**) with neutral detergent; press-wash. In the machine: **net, gentle cycle, no spin** |
| **Cotton canvas (totes)** | **Yes**, but expect **shrinkage and colour loss** | Wash alone. Unbleached canvas resists sun-yellowing, but dry coloured bags in the shade |
| **Real leather or synthetic leather (PU) handles or base** | **No** | Wipe with a damp cloth, then a dry one. Condition leather with a leather cream |
| **Water-repellent or waterproof finish** | Washing **weakens the finish** | After drying, apply a **water-repellent spray** (撥水スプレー, hassui supurē; outdoors, avoiding fittings and leather) |
| **Bags with stiffeners or a base board** | **Mostly no** | Distortion and glue coming apart. Remove the board and wash if it comes out |
| **Randoseru (school satchel)** | **No** | Wipe with a dry or lightly damp cloth. Follow the label for dedicated cleaners |

- **Before washing**: empty it completely, remove **grit from the bottom of the pockets**, **close the zips**, and **wrap the metal fittings in a towel** or cover them with cloth to protect both the fabric and the machine.
- **Spot cleaning (handles, shoulder straps)**: **dab with an old toothbrush dipped in diluted neutral detergent**, then wipe off with a wet towel. Often this saves you washing the whole bag.
- **Drying**: **hang it upside down** so the water drains out, then leave it **in an airy, shaded place for about two days.** Incomplete drying leads to **mould and smells.** Hairdryers and tumble dryers damage the fabric and the glue.
- **If you use the machine**: **no spin**, or **30 seconds at most.** Spinning is the main cause of distortion and broken fittings.
- **If only the smell bothers you**: leave **a sachet of baking soda (重曹, jūsō)** or a **desiccant** inside overnight, or turn it inside out and give it a short spell in the sun.
- **Bags with no care label** (sold as general goods, not clothing) — check the **maker's care instructions.** If in doubt, **test water and detergent on a hidden spot on the back** first.

> **[Verify]** The water tolerance of each material and the weakening of water-repellent finishes are general explanations. Check them against bag-industry bodies and the care-label documents.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Just put it through a normal machine cycle

**You get distortion, broken fittings, a weakened water-repellent finish and scratches on the drum.** Hand-wash as a rule; in the machine, gentle cycle and no spin.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The leather handles can go in the wash too

**Water makes leather stiffen, lose colour and crack.** If a bag has leather parts, do not wash the whole bag; wipe it instead.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Dry it in the sun so it dries faster

**Direct sun fades colours and degrades the fabric**, and synthetic leather goes hard. Dry slowly in the shade.

## Misunderstanding 4｜A water-repellent bag stays water-repellent after washing

**Detergent and friction strip the finish.** Re-apply a water-repellent spray after drying.

## Misunderstanding 5｜A randoseru can be washed all over in water too

**A randoseru is a composite of stiffeners, leather and synthetic leather, and must not be washed in water.** Wipe only. It is built to last six years of school, so follow the maker's care method.
