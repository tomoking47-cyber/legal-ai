# Q. Make-up removal wipes: is it all right to use them every day?｜Japan Questions #529

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/makeup-removal-wipes` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

You come home exhausted and the cleansing wipe saves you a trip to the bathroom. Handy for travel and the gym too, and Japanese drugstores (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.) sell them in big value packs. You may also have heard that "they are bad for your skin". Here we look at what the actual problem is, and how to use them with the least strain on the skin.

## A. A cleansing wipe removes make-up by rubbing, and its biggest weakness is that it causes more friction on the skin than a rinse-off cleanser. Daily use can lead to pigmentation (brown under-eye circles, dullness) and dryness, so treat wipes as a product for "the nights you really cannot manage" and for travel. When you do use one: ① hold it against the skin for a few seconds to loosen the make-up first; ② glide it in one direction without pressure; ③ use a separate wipe or cotton buds for eye make-up; ④ afterwards, rinse with lukewarm water or at least wipe off the residue with toner, then moisturise. The point is to avoid leaving the wipe's cleansing agents and alcohol on the skin.

## What a cleansing wipe is

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **How it works** | Non-woven fabric soaked in **cleansing agents (surfactants), humectants, and water or alcohol**, which **physically wipes make-up off.** A rinse-off cleanser "dissolves and rinses"; a wipe "dissolves and wipes" |
| **Biggest weakness** | **Friction.** Facial skin, especially around the eyes (about 0.5 mm), is thin, and repeated friction causes **pigmentation, fine lines and redness** |
| **Second weakness** | **Cleansing agents stay on the skin.** If you do not rinse, surfactants, alcohol and preservatives remain and cause dryness and irritation |
| **Good for** | **Travel and business trips, the gym, camping, disasters**, and **nights when you are too tired to stand at the sink** |
| **Not suited to daily use by** | **People with sensitive or dry skin, a lot of acne, or pigmentation around the eyes** |
| **How often** | **Up to once or twice a week** as a guide. When you do use one, **do not scrub the whole face with one wipe**; **change the surface for T-zone → cheeks → eyes** |

- **Steps**: ① **Hold the wipe against the face for a few seconds** so the make-up loosens (do not wipe straight away) ② Glide lightly **inwards to outwards, bottom to top, in one direction** ③ **Change the surface, or use a second wipe, for eye make-up and lipstick.** For the eyes, **press and wait 5–10 seconds, then glide gently over the lid** ④ **Then rinse with lukewarm water** (a face wash is often unnecessary) ⑤ **Moisturise.**
- **Alcohol (ethanol)**: often added for a cooling feel and quick drying, and **irritating to sensitive skin.** Choose wipes labelled **"alcohol-free" (アルコールフリー).**
- **Storage**: **as the wipes dry out, the cleansing agents become more concentrated and more irritating**, and they clean less well. **Close the lid firmly after opening and use up within 1–2 months.**
- **Heavy make-up (waterproof mascara, tint lipstick)** does not come off easily with a wipe and **leads to over-rubbing.** Use a dedicated remover as well.
- **About "no rinsing needed" labels**: **even if the label says rinsing is not required, most dermatologists take the view that the skin is better off without residue.** Rinse if you can.
- **In a disaster**: when water is unavailable, wipes are **an effective way to keep the skin clean.** They are worth keeping in your emergency kit (防災バッグ, bōsai baggu — many Japanese households keep one).

> **[Verify]** The skin-thickness figure and the guide to shelf life after opening are general values. Follow the labelling on each product.

## Misunderstanding 1｜It says "no rinsing needed", so I can wipe and go to bed

**Cleansing agents remain on the skin and cause dryness and irritation.** Rinse with lukewarm water if you can; at the least, wipe off with toner and then moisturise.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Wipes are convenient, so daily is fine

**Accumulated friction can cause pigmentation and dullness.** Keep them for "the nights you really cannot manage" and travel.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Rub hard to get everything off

**Friction is the biggest weakness.** Hold for a few seconds to loosen, then glide lightly in one direction. If it will not come off, use a dedicated remover.

## Misunderstanding 4｜One wipe for the whole face is economical

**Scrubbing with the same surface spreads the removed make-up around and adds friction.** Change the surface; do the eyes separately.

## Misunderstanding 5｜A pack opened six months ago is still fine

**Dried-out wipes have more concentrated ingredients, are more irritating, and may carry bacteria.** Buy a pack size you will use up within 1–2 months of opening.
