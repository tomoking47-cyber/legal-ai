# Q. Do face mists moisturise, or do they dry your skin out?｜Japan Questions #562

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/face-mist-sprays` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Face mists (ミスト化粧水, a spray version of the watery Japanese "lotion" step) have become a handbag staple in Japan. They promise a quick spritz against daytime dryness and moisture over make-up. But you also hear that "spraying alone dries the skin". This article looks at how water actually behaves on skin.

## A. If you spray a mist and leave it, the droplets take some of your skin's own moisture with them as they evaporate, so on balance the skin can end up slightly drier than if you had done nothing (especially in an air-conditioned room). Whether a mist moisturises depends on **the ingredients** and **what you do next**: ① products containing humectants such as glycerin, hyaluronic acid or ceramides, or some oil, hold water on the skin; ② press the mist in gently with your palms after spraying; ③ where possible, seal with a milky emulsion (乳液, nyūeki) or cream. Water-only mists (hot-spring water, purified water) give a cooling feel and a moment of moisture, but the moisture does not last. Over make-up, use a fine-particle mist held 20–30 cm away.

## Types of mist and what they do

| Type | Ingredients | How long the moisture lasts | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Water-based mist (hot-spring water, purified water, spray versions of lotion)** | Water plus a little humectant | **Short** (a few minutes to ten-odd minutes). Left alone, tends to dry | **Cooling, a moment of moisture after washing, blending primer before make-up.** Press in and layer afterwards |
| **Humectant mist** | **Glycerin, BG (butylene glycol), hyaluronic acid, ceramides, amino acids** | **Medium.** Stays on the skin and holds water | **Daytime dryness, air-conditioned rooms.** Press in |
| **Oil-in mist (two-layer)** | Water layer plus oil layer (squalane, jojoba oil, etc.). Shake before use | **Long.** The oil slows evaporation | **Daytime moisture for dry skin, a glow over make-up.** Use sparingly if you shine easily |
| **Make-up fixing mist** | Film-forming polymers, alcohol | Not moisture but **holding make-up in place** | Finishing make-up. **The alcohol dries some people out** |

- **Why it can dry the skin**: water droplets on the skin **take heat and moisture from their surroundings as they evaporate** (latent heat). **Without humectants or oil, water in the outer skin layer evaporates along with them.** This is the real story behind "mists dry you out", and it comes from **spraying plain water and leaving it**.
- **Basic technique**: ① **hold 20–30 cm away, just enough for a fine haze to settle over the whole face** (too close forms droplets and shifts make-up) ② **press in gently with your palms** (do not rub) ③ **on very dry days, seal with emulsion, cream or an oil-in mist** ④ **no more than 2–3 times a day** (repeatedly wetting and drying the skin is a strain).
- **Over make-up**: choose a **fine-particle mist** and **press lightly with a tissue afterwards so the make-up holds.** Suits **the afternoon, when foundation starts to look powdery**.
- **Air-conditioned rooms and aeroplanes**: at **20–30% humidity** water evaporates quickly. Choose **a humectant or oil-in mist** and press it in. On flights, **a mask plus mist** is the standard combination.
- **Sensitive skin**: pick products **low in alcohol (ethanol) and fragrance.** Strongly "cooling" products usually contain more alcohol.
- **Storage**: aim to use up **within 3–6 months of opening.** **Spray bottles rarely let germs flow back in**, but wipe the nozzle. **Do not leave in a hot car.**
- **Do-it-yourself version**: decanting your usual lotion into a spray bottle is possible, but **the preservative system was not designed for another container, so use it up within 1–2 weeks** to be safe.

> **[Verify]** The explanation of drying through latent heat is the general account given in dermatology and cosmetic science. Check against the usage cautions of the Japan Cosmetic Industry Association.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Just spraying a mist moisturises

**A water-only mist left to evaporate takes skin moisture with it.** Choose one with humectants, press it in, and seal if needed.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The more you spray, the more moisture you get

**Droplets form, make-up shifts and more water evaporates.** Hold 20–30 cm away for a light haze, up to 2–3 times a day.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Anything is fine over make-up

**Coarse-particle mists form droplets and ruin make-up.** Choose a fine-particle mist and press lightly with a tissue.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Hot-spring water mists have special effects

**Most of the content is water and the effect of the minerals is limited.** Use it as a tool for a cooling feel and a moment of moisture.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Decanting your lotion into a spray bottle is the same thing

**The preservative design is different, so a decanted product has to be used up within 1–2 weeks.** If you use a mist often, a purpose-made product is more hygienic.
