# Q. Spots on my back and chest — should I care for them the same way as my face?｜Japan Questions #565

- Shelf: `beauty` ／ URL: `/en/beauty/back-and-body-acne` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

You want to wear something backless in the Japanese summer, but your back is covered in bumps. Spots on the back, chest and shoulders appear where the skin is oily, just as on the face. But sometimes the cause is not acne at all, and face-style care then does nothing. This article explains how to tell the difference and what to do.

## A. Some of the "acne" on people's backs is not acne (Cutibacterium acnes) but **Malassezia folliculitis**, caused by a yeast that lives naturally on the skin. It looks similar, yet acne medicines do not help it. If small, uniform bumps spread with itching, Malassezia is a possibility, and the quickest route is an antifungal medicine prescribed at a dermatology clinic (covered by Japanese health insurance). If it is acne: ① wash your body after shampoo and conditioner (conditioner residue runs down the back), ② shower or change after sweating, ③ do not scrub with a nylon wash towel, ④ wear cotton underlayers, ⑤ moisturise with low-oil products, ⑥ try a medicated (薬用) anti-acne body wash of quasi-drug grade (a Japanese category between cosmetics and medicines). If you want to avoid marks, see a dermatologist early.

## Telling the bumps apart

| Condition | Appearance | Itch | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Acne (acne vulgaris)** | **Mixed sizes**: whiteheads, blackheads, red bumps and pus-filled spots together | Little | Shoulders, upper back, chest | Manage sebum and sweat, OTC acne medicines, prescription topicals from a dermatologist |
| **Malassezia folliculitis** | **Many small, uniform red bumps** with a **shine** | **Yes** (mild) | Back, chest, shoulders; **sweaty summers and after exercise** | **Antifungal medicine (prescribed by a dermatologist).** Acne medicines do not work |
| **Keratosis pilaris (さめ肌, "shark skin")** | Fine, rough, skin-coloured to red bumps, **no pain** | None | Upper arms, back, thighs | Moisturise (urea or salicylic-acid products). Constitutional, common in teens, eases in adulthood |
| **Contact dermatitis (rash)** | Redness and small blisters **matching the area of contact** | **Strong** | Detergent, fabric softener, body wash, metal fasteners on underwear | Remove the cause |
| **Heat rash (あせも, miliaria)** | Small red bumps **right after sweating** | Itchy, prickly | Neck, back, armpits | Cool down and rinse off the sweat |

## What to do if it is acne

| Measure | Details |
|---|---|
| **Washing order** | **Shampoo → conditioner → body.** Conditioner residue left on the back clogs pores. **Clip your hair up before washing your body** |
| **How to wash** | **Lather soap or body wash and use your hands or a cotton cloth.** **Scrubbing with a nylon wash towel (ナイロンタオル, the long scrubbing cloth sold in Japan) damages the outer skin layer and makes things worse.** **Medicated (薬用, quasi-drug) anti-acne body washes** carry the approved claim "prevents acne" |
| **Sweat** | **Shower after sweating, or wipe down and change.** **Do not stay in sports kit or sweat-soaked underlayers for long.** **If you sweat heavily at night, keep bedding clean** |
| **Clothing and bedding** | **Cotton underlayers**, **sheets and pillowcases washed weekly**, **fragrance in fabric softener can irritate** |
| **Moisturising** | **Dryness also causes spots.** **Low-oil lotions and gels** (labelled non-comedogenic) |
| **OTC medicines** | **Acne medicines (ibuprofen piconol, sulphur, etc.)**, sold at drugstores (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, etc.). **The back is hard to reach yourself**, so spray types exist |
| **Dermatologist** | **Many red spots, marks starting to form, no improvement after 1–2 months of OTC care** → prescriptions such as **adapalene, benzoyl peroxide, topical antibiotics**. **The back is an area where marks (darkening, scars) tend to stay** |
| **UV** | Sunburn on the back **deepens dark marks.** Use sunscreen or cover with clothing |

- **Spotting Malassezia folliculitis matters**: **a month of acne medicine with no change, uniform bumps, itching** → dermatologist. **Topical antifungals usually bring improvement in 2–4 weeks.** **It comes back easily**, so keep managing sweat the same way.
- **Diet**: as with facial acne, **no direct link to specific foods is established**, though some reports link it to high-sugar diets.
- **Stress, sleep and the menstrual cycle** make it worse for some people (hormonal influence).
- **Marks on the back (darkening)**: **usually fade over several months to a year.** **Brightening (bihaku) active ingredients of quasi-drug grade plus UV protection** help. **Indented scars do not respond to cosmetics and belong to cosmetic dermatology (self-pay).**
- **Clothing plans**: **if you have a backless outfit or swimwear coming up, start 2–3 months ahead.** It does not settle overnight.

> **[Verify]** Check the description and management of Malassezia folliculitis, and the medicines recommended in the acne guideline, against Japanese Dermatological Association material.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Every bump on the back is acne

**Malassezia folliculitis (a skin yeast), keratosis pilaris, contact rashes and heat rash are all mixed in.** If a month of acne medicine changes nothing, have a dermatologist tell them apart.

## Misunderstanding 2｜A good scrub with a nylon towel clears the pores

**It damages the outer skin layer and makes things worse.** Lather and hands, plus attention to conditioner residue, work better.

## Misunderstanding 3｜The back does not get dry, so it needs no moisturiser

**Dryness is one cause of spots.** Moisturise with a low-oil lotion or gel.

## Misunderstanding 4｜A tan dries the spots out and clears them

**UV worsens both the inflammation and the dark marks.** Sunscreen on the back, or cover up.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Marks fade on their own if left alone

**Red and brown marks fade, but indented scars do not respond to cosmetics.** Seeing a dermatologist early is the surest way to avoid marks.
