# Q. Clothes I bought online don't fit. Can I return them? Is there a cooling-off period?｜Japan Questions #571

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/online-clothing-returns` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Most of us buy clothes online now, but whether you can send back an item that is the wrong size or colour depends on the shop. Many people in Japan assume that online purchases carry a legal "cooling-off" right. They do not. Here are the rules that actually apply, and how to buy so that you need to return less often.

## A. Online shopping (通信販売, "mail-order sales" in Japanese law) has no statutory cooling-off period. Whether you can return goods is governed by the shop's published returns policy (返品特約, henpin tokuyaku). Under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (特定商取引法, Japan's consumer law for distance and door-to-door selling), only when a shop displays no returns policy do you have a legal right to return goods within 8 days of delivery, paying the return postage yourself. Most large retailers accept returns that are "unused, tags attached, within 7–30 days of delivery"; postage is often free for a size exchange but charged for a change of mind. Underwear, swimwear and sale items are often non-returnable. To reduce failures: (1) note the actual measurements of clothes you already own (shoulder width, chest width, body length, sleeve length) and compare them with the product's measurement chart; (2) read reviews for comments on sizing; (3) check the returns policy before you order.

## Return rules

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Cooling-off** | **There is no statutory cooling-off period for online / mail-order sales** (it applies to door-to-door and telephone sales). "You can cool off on online purchases" is a misunderstanding |
| **Returns policy (返品特約)** | **The return conditions displayed by each shop take priority** (Act on Specified Commercial Transactions, Articles 11 and 15-3). **Check the "Returns" page and the "特定商取引法に基づく表記" (legal disclosure page) before ordering** |
| **If no policy is displayed** | **You may return within 8 days of delivery, paying the return postage yourself** (the statutory return right) |
| **Typical conditions** | **Unused, tags attached, accessories included, within 7–30 days of delivery**. **Trying on is fine; washed, worn or smelling items are not accepted** is the norm |
| **Often non-returnable** | **Underwear, swimwear, socks, sale items, made-to-order, shipped from overseas, personalised items** |
| **Postage** | **Size or colour exchange free; change-of-mind returns charged (¥500–1,000); defective or wrong items at the shop's cost** is common |
| **Defective or wrong item** | **Regardless of the returns policy, you can demand an exchange or refund for non-conformity with the contract** (Civil Code). Take photos and contact the shop |
| **Overseas shops** | **Japanese law often does not apply**, and return postage can exceed the price of the item. **Customs duty and consumption tax** are charged separately too |

## Reducing sizing mistakes

| Step | Details |
|---|---|
| **1. Measure clothes you own** | Lay a favourite garment flat and note **shoulder width, chest width (half the circumference), body length, sleeve length**; for trousers **waist, hips, front rise, inseam, thigh width, hem width** |
| **2. Compare with the product's "actual measurements"** | **Look at the measurement chart, not "M" or "size 9"**. **The same S can differ by 2–5 cm between brands**. Do not confuse **"nude" measurements (your body) with garment measurements (the clothes)** (see our article on size labels) |
| **3. Reviews** | Comments on fit and fabric such as **"I'm usually an M but this runs large" or "the fabric is thin"**. **Reviews that give height and weight** are the most useful |
| **4. Fabric and stretch** | **All-cotton fabric does not stretch; polyurethane blends stretch; knits stretch**. Allow for **shrinkage in the wash** (cotton, linen and rayon shrink) |
| **5. Try-on services** | **Try at home (Amazon Prime Try Before You Buy, ZOZO's free-return period)**; brands that offer **collection and returns in store** |
| **6. Read the returns policy before ordering** | **Deadline, postage, condition requirements, refund method (some shops refund only in points)** |

- **Try-on etiquette**: **do not remove tags; avoid getting make-up or perfume on the garment; try indoors for a short time; do not walk on the floor in shoes**. Some people **order several sizes intending to return the rest**, but some shops have **restricted the accounts of customers with high return rates**.
- **How to return**: **apply through the shop's My Page or contact address → follow the return instructions → post within the deadline → refund (1–2 weeks)**. Check **whether cash-on-delivery return postage is accepted, and keep the tracking number**. **Parcels sent back without applying first may be refused**.
- **Flea-market apps and private sales (Mercari etc.)**: **returns are not accepted in principle** ("no claims, no returns"). **If the item differs from the description or is faulty, go through the platform's support office**.
- **Subscriptions and repeat purchases**: many disputes arise where **a "first order free" offer turns into a recurring contract**. **Check the cancellation terms**; **the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions requires a final confirmation screen** (2021 amendment, in force from 1 June 2022).
- **If you are stuck**: call the **Consumer Affairs Centre hotline (188)** (消費生活センター, the local consumer advice service). A shop **refusing returns without displaying a returns policy**, or **refusing to exchange a faulty item**, is a valid subject for advice.

> **[Verify]** Check the statutory return right (8 days) under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions, the duty to display a returns policy, and the 2022 amendment's final-confirmation-screen rule against Consumer Affairs Agency materials.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Online purchases have a cooling-off period

**There is no statutory cooling-off for mail-order sales.** Whether you can return depends on the shop's returns policy. Check before ordering.

## Misunderstanding 2｜If the size is wrong I can always return it

**Change-of-mind returns follow the shop's rules.** Only where no policy is displayed can you return within 8 days at your own postage cost.

## Misunderstanding 3｜An "M" is the same as another brand's "M"

**The same label can differ by 2–5 cm in actual measurements.** Compare the measurements of clothes you own with the product's measurement chart.

## Misunderstanding 4｜If it says "no returns", I have to accept a faulty item

**For faulty or wrong items you can demand an exchange or refund regardless of the returns policy.** Take photos and contact the shop; if they refuse, call the Consumer Affairs Centre.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Overseas shops accept returns just like Japanese ones

**Japanese law does not reach them, and return postage and customs duty often leave you out of pocket.** Avoid buying with a return in mind.
