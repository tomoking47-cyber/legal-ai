# Q. My synthetic-leather bag or shoes are peeling and sticky. How long do they last? Can it be prevented?｜Japan Questions #573

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/synthetic-leather-lifespan` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

A bag you bought only two or three years ago has a surface that has gone sticky and started to peel. This is "hydrolysis" (加水分解, kasui bunkai) of synthetic leather (合皮, gōhi). Synthetic leather is cheaper and lighter than real leather, but many people buy it without knowing it has a built-in lifespan, and the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan receives complaints about it. Here is how it happens, and how to make it last a little longer.

## A. Most synthetic leather is polyurethane (PU) coated onto a fabric backing. From the day it is made it reacts with moisture in the air and its molecules break apart — hydrolysis — so after 2–5 years the surface goes sticky, cracks and peels. This depends on age, not on how often you use it; it continues even in storage, and Japan's hot, humid climate speeds it up. It cannot be stopped. To slow it down: store in a well-ventilated place (plastic bags, sealed containers and the back of a closet make it worse); wipe off moisture and sweat; avoid direct sun and heat; use the item now and then so that it gets air. PVC (vinyl) synthetic leather hydrolyses less but goes hard and sticky; real leather lasts 10 years or more with care. For anything you want to keep, the realistic choice is real leather or PU labelled as hydrolysis-resistant.

## Types of synthetic leather and their lifespans

| Material | Label | Character | Typical lifespan | How it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Polyurethane (PU) synthetic leather** | "合成皮革" (synthetic leather), "ポリウレタン" (polyurethane) | **Light, soft, close to real leather in feel**. Most bags, shoes and sofas | **2–5 years** (from manufacture) | **Hydrolysis**: sticky → cracks → peels. **Progresses even unused** |
| **PVC (vinyl) synthetic leather** | "合成皮革", "塩化ビニル" (PVC) | Firmer, cheap, **water-resistant** | **5–10 years** | Hardens; plasticiser leaches out, leaving stickiness and cracks. **Goes stiff in the cold** |
| **Artificial leather (microfibre type)** | "人工皮革" (artificial leather) (Clarino, Ecsaine etc.) | **Non-woven fabric impregnated with PU**. Common in randoseru school satchels. **Designed to resist hydrolysis** | **6–10 years** (randoseru usually carry a 6-year warranty) | Surface wear |
| **Real leather** | "牛革" (cowhide), "羊革" (sheepskin) etc. | Needs care, heavy, expensive | **10 years to decades** | Drying out and cracking (preventable with care) |

## Slowing hydrolysis

| Measure | Why |
|---|---|
| **Store in a well-ventilated place** | **Humidity drives the reaction**. **Long spells in a plastic bag, sealed case, the back of a closet or a box** are the worst conditions |
| **Use it** | **Taking it out now and then so it gets air and dries** usually makes it last longer than putting it away |
| **Wipe off moisture** | Wipe off **rain, sweat and skin oils** before storing. **Leaving it wet** speeds things up |
| **Avoid heat and direct sun** | **Inside a car, by a window, near a heater** all accelerate deterioration |
| **Stuffing in storage** | **Newspaper** to keep the shape (it also absorbs moisture). **A dehumidifier pack nearby** (but do not seal) |
| **Dedicated protector** | **Protective spray for synthetic leather** to guard the surface. **Oils and creams made for real leather may not suit PU** (stickiness) |
| **When buying** | **Old stock (outlets, sales) may already have deteriorated** before you buy. Choose **PU labelled "hydrolysis-resistant", artificial leather or PVC** |

- **Once hydrolysis has started**: **there is no way back**. Stickiness **comes from inside, so wiping the surface does not help**. **Patching peeled areas (synthetic-leather repair sheets)** is a cosmetic first-aid measure; it does not stop the process. **Hydrolysis of PU shoe soles** can be dealt with by replacing the sole (see our article on shoe repair).
- **Shoes with PU soles**: **the midsoles of hiking boots and trainers** are the classic case; the National Consumer Affairs Center has reports of **soles disintegrating on the day someone put on five-year-old shoes**. **Press the sole of old shoes to check before wearing them**.
- **Material labelling**: **bags and handbags must carry material labelling such as "合成皮革" (synthetic leather) or "人工皮革" (artificial leather) under the Miscellaneous Manufactured Goods Quality Labelling Regulations of the Household Goods Quality Labelling Act (家庭用品品質表示法, Japan's consumer labelling law)**. **Footwear is not a covered product under that Act**; shoe labelling rests mainly on the industry's **Fair Competition Code on Shoe Labelling (靴の表示に関する公正競争規約)**. **Wording that makes a product look like real leather is a matter for the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations (景品表示法, misleading superiority)**. Check the label when you buy.
- **Price versus lifespan**: **a ¥5,000 synthetic bag replaced every 3 years**, or **a ¥30,000 real-leather bag used for 10 years**? **Daily use, long-term use → real leather**; **fashion pieces, occasional use, lightness first → synthetic**.
- **Disposal**: in most municipalities it goes in **burnable waste** (once metal fittings are removed); **large items are bulky waste (粗大ごみ, sodai gomi)**.

> **[Verify]** The years quoted for hydrolysis and the lifespan of artificial leather are guidelines based on explanations from material manufacturers and the National Consumer Affairs Center. Check the labelling rules against the Miscellaneous Manufactured Goods Quality Labelling Regulations under the Household Goods Quality Labelling Act.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Synthetic leather lasts longer if you keep it carefully put away

**Hydrolysis continues even when unused, and sealed storage speeds it up through humidity.** A ventilated spot and occasional use last longer.

## Misunderstanding 2｜The stickiness is dirt, so wiping will fix it

**It is internal deterioration coming to the surface, and wiping does not reverse it.** Consider it the end of its life; for shoe soles, look at a sole replacement.

## Misunderstanding 3｜Real-leather cream will make it last

**Oils for real leather do not suit PU and can cause stickiness.** Use a synthetic-leather protector, or just wipe with a dry cloth.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Sale and outlet synthetic leather is the same quality

**Stock that has sat since manufacture may already be deteriorating.** Check the surface for stickiness and softness before you buy.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Synthetic leather is fake leather and poor quality

**Synthetic leather wins in many situations on weight, price and water resistance.** The difference is a fixed lifespan. Choose by use.
