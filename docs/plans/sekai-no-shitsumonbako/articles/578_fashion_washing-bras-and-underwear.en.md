# Q. How should I wash bras and underwear so they last? When should I replace them?｜Japan Questions #578

- Shelf: `fashion` ／ URL: `/en/fashion/washing-bras-and-underwear` ／ Status: **Draft — English edition, sources unverified**

Underwear is worn every day, yet its care tends to come last. A bra thrown straight into the machine and its wire bent, elastic that has gone slack, yellowing — all common failures. Here is how to wash underwear, and its surprisingly short lifespan. Where a Japanese product name helps you find it in a shop, we give it.

## A. For a bra: fasten the hooks, shape the cups, put it in a bra-shaped (3D) laundry net, wash on a gentle or delicates cycle, spin briefly, then reshape the cups and hang it upside down (straps down) or dry it flat. Never tumble dry — heat wrecks the elastic and wires. If hand-washing, press gently in water below 30°C (86°F). Knickers and socks can go in a normal wash, but a net reduces tangling and stretching. Lifespan: a bra worn 2–3 times a week lasts 6 months to a year (the band stretches and rides up, wires poke out, cups gape); knickers 6 months to a year; socks until they wear thin. Yellowing is oxidised skin oil and fades with a soak in oxygen bleach at 40°C (104°F).

## How to wash

| Item | Washing | Drying | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bra (underwired)** | **Fasten the hooks** (so they do not snag other clothes) → **bra net (3D type, ブラジャー用ネット)** (1–2 bras at most) → **gentle / delicates cycle, neutral detergent** → **spin 30 seconds to 1 minute**. Hand-wash: **press gently below 30°C; do not knead the cups** | **Shape the cups, peg by the band and hang upside down**, or **dry flat**. **Hanging by the straps stretches the elastic**. In the shade | **Tumble dryer** (distorts elastic, wires and cups); **loose in a normal cycle** (wires bend or poke out); **hot water**; **chlorine bleach** |
| **Non-wired bras, bra tops** | Net, gentle cycle | Dry flat | Tumble dryer |
| **Knickers** | **Normal cycle in a net** is fine (stops tangling and stretching). **Pre-rinse menstrual blood and discharge in cold water** (hot water sets the protein) | In the shade (**sunlight degrades fabric and elastic and fades colour**) | Chlorine bleach (colours and patterns) |
| **Socks** | **Turn inside out, in a net** (skin oil and dead skin are on the inside); normal cycle | Peg with **the toes up** (so water does not pool in the elastic) | — |
| **Shapewear, girdles** | **Hand-wash or gentle cycle**, net | Dry flat | Tumble dryer, heat |
| **Silk and lace underwear** | **Hand-wash, neutral detergent, below 30°C** | Shade; dry flat | Machine spin, sunlight |

## How long they last

| Item | Guide | Signs to replace |
|---|---|---|
| **Bra** | **Worn 2–3 times a week: 6 months to 1 year** (around **100 wears**) | **Band elastic stretched so the back rides up**; **cups gape or collapse**; **wires poke out or dig in**; **straps loose even at the shortest setting**; **fabric worn thin** |
| **Knickers** | **6 months to 1 year** | Loose elastic, thin fabric, yellowing or stains that will not come out |
| **Socks** | **When the heel wears thin** (a few months to a year) | See-through, stretched elastic, holes |
| **Undershirts / base layers** | **1–2 years** | Stretched, yellowed, smelly |

- **Yellowing**: **oxidised skin oil and sweat**. **Dissolve oxygen bleach (酸素系漂白剤, sanso-kei hyōhakuzai) in 40°C water and soak for 30 minutes to 1 hour** → wash (**after a colour test**). **Chlorine bleach damages fabric and elastic**, so do not use it. **Washing straight after wearing** is the prevention.
- **Odour**: **not fully dried**, or **built-up skin oil**. **Dry thoroughly, soak in oxygen bleach, and if the label allows around 60°C, wash hot**.
- **Re-check your size**: **bust size changes with weight, age, pregnancy and breastfeeding**. **Try on and measure once a year**. An ill-fitting bra is generally described as a cause of **shoulder stiffness and loss of shape**.
- **Rotation**: **rotating 5–7 bras** reduces the load on each one and rests the elastic. **Do not wear the same bra on consecutive days**.
- **Storage**: **do not crush the cups** (stack them or stand them up); **fasten the hooks**.
- **Children and juniors**: during growth, **check the size every 3–6 months**.
- **Care labels**: **underwear has care labels too**. Check for **"hand wash", "machine wash gentle" and "do not bleach"**.

> **[Verify]** Bra lifespan (number of wears) is a guideline based on guidance from lingerie makers. Check how to read care labels against Consumer Affairs Agency materials.

## Misunderstanding 1｜Bras can go straight into the washing machine

**Wires bend or poke out, cups distort, and they tangle with other clothes.** Fasten the hooks and use a bra net.

## Misunderstanding 2｜Tumble-drying underwear is hygienic and quick

**Heat distorts the elastic, wires and cups and cuts the lifespan sharply.** Dry in the shade or flat.

## Misunderstanding 3｜A bra lasts for years

**The guide is around 100 wears, or 6 months to a year at 2–3 wears a week.** A band that rides up or cups that gape mean it is time to replace.

## Misunderstanding 4｜Use chlorine bleach to get rid of yellowing

**It damages fabric and elastic.** Soak in oxygen bleach at 40°C. Prevention is washing straight after wearing.

## Misunderstanding 5｜Once the size fits, it never changes

**It changes with weight, age and pregnancy.** Try on and measure once a year.
