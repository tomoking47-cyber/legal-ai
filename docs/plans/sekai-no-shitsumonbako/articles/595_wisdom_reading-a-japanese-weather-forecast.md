# Q. What do people get wrong about reading a Japanese weather forecast?｜Japanese Everyday Wisdom #595

- Shelf: `wisdom` ／ URL: `/en/wisdom/reading-a-japanese-weather-forecast` ／ Status: **Draft — sources unverified**

Japanese weather forecasts are precise in ways that surprise newcomers — a "probability of precipitation" that is not what it seems, a laundry index, a pollen level, an "umbrella needed" icon, and heat-stroke alerts — and they shape daily behaviour: whether to hang laundry outside, carry an umbrella, or send a child to school in a rain cape. Learning to read them is a domestic skill.

## A. The common misunderstanding is that "降水確率 50%" means half the day will be rainy. It means that in similar forecast situations, 1 mm or more of rain fell at some point in the period in 50% of cases — nothing about duration or amount. Japanese households act on it with thresholds: 30% or above and you carry a folding umbrella; 50% and you don't hang laundry outside; 70% and you plan indoors. Read alongside it the hourly precipitation map (雨雲レーダー, radar), the laundry index (洗濯指数), the UV and heat-stroke indices, and the warnings hierarchy (注意報 advisory → 警報 warning → 特別警報 emergency warning), which decides whether schools close.

## The numbers and what they mean

| Item | Japanese | What it actually means | Household threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Probability of precipitation** | **降水確率** | **Chance that ≥1 mm of rain (or snow) falls somewhere in the area during the 6-hour period**. Issued in 10% steps. **Not duration, not amount** | **≥30%: folding umbrella. ≥50%: laundry inside. ≥70%: indoor plans** |
| **Rain radar / nowcast** | **雨雲レーダー・ナウキャスト** | JMA's 5-minute-updated radar with **1-hour (and 15-hour) forecast** of rain intensity | The tool for "can I make it to the station before it hits" |
| **Laundry index** | **洗濯指数** (Japan Weather Association, tenki.jp) | 0–100 scale combining humidity, sun, wind, rain chance: **≥70 "dries well", 30–60 "part-dry", <30 "indoor"** | Decides morning laundry for millions |
| **Heat-stroke alert** | **熱中症警戒アラート** | Issued when the **WBGT (暑さ指数) is forecast at 33+** | Cancel outdoor activity; check on the elderly (see the heatstroke article) |
| **UV index** | **UV指数** | 1–11+; **8+ is very strong** | Sunscreen, hat |
| **Pollen** | **花粉** | 5 levels from 少ない to 非常に多い | Windows shut, laundry in |
| **Discomfort / "sensible temperature"** | **体感温度・不快指数** | Includes wind and humidity | — |
| **Snow** | **降雪量・積雪** | cm of new snow / snow depth | Trains and roads |

## The warning hierarchy (what closes schools)

| Level | Japanese | Meaning | Typical response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advisory** | **注意報** | Possible damage (heavy rain, strong wind, thunder, dry air, frost, dense fog) | Carry on, be careful |
| **Warning** | **警報** | Serious damage likely (heavy rain, flood, storm, heavy snow, high waves, storm surge) | **Many schools close or delay if a 警報 (esp. heavy rain/storm) is in force at 07:00**; companies allow late arrival |
| **Emergency warning** | **特別警報** | **Once-in-decades danger**; evacuate | Follow the municipality's evacuation levels |
| **Evacuation levels** | **警戒レベル 1–5** | 3: elderly evacuate; **4: everyone evacuate**; 5: disaster under way | Set by the municipality via area mail (エリアメール) |

- **Where to read**: **JMA (気象庁) site/app** — the official source, sparse design; **tenki.jp (Japan Weather Association)** — indices, pollen, laundry; **Weathernews** — crowdsourced rain reports and the "5-minute rain" alerts; **Yahoo! 天気** — the most-used app, with the rain radar and push alerts; **NHK** for warnings; **NERV (特務機関NERV)** — the disaster-alert app popular for its speed.
- **The 6-hour blocks**: forecasts split the day into **0–6, 6–12, 12–18, 18–24**; **"afternoon 60%" is not "evening 60%"** — check the block you need.
- **Typhoon reading**: the **forecast circle (予報円)** is where the centre may be, not the storm's size; **the storm area (暴風域, red) and strong-wind area (強風域, yellow)** are what matter; **"上陸" (landfall)** timing decides train stoppages (**計画運休, planned suspensions**) announced a day ahead.
- **Snow in Tokyo**: **a 5 cm forecast stops trains and empties convenience-store bread shelves**; treat any Tokyo snow forecast as a transport day off.
- **Area mail (エリアメール / 緊急速報)**: **the loud alarm on all phones** for earthquakes (緊急地震速報), tsunami, evacuation, missile (J-Alert). Not switchable off for the earthquake/tsunami tier on Japanese SIMs; foreign SIMs may not receive it — install NERV or Yahoo! 防災.
- **Seasonal words to know**: **梅雨入り/明け** (rainy season start/end), **猛暑日** (35°C+ day), **真夏日** (30°C+), **熱帯夜** (night above 25°C), **木枯らし1号** (first cold north wind), **花粉飛散開始**.
