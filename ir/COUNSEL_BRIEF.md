## B. Questions for counsel — please verify on EDGAR and advise

### B-1. Section 13(d) — Schedule 13D
The Company understands no Schedule 13D/A has been filed. Please confirm and determine:

1. Has **any** Schedule 13D or 13G been filed by any Relativity entity **with respect to
   BIOT** (CUSIP G4860C107), as distinct from the predecessor SPAC, Relativity
   Acquisition Corp (CIK 1860484)?
2. If no initial Schedule 13D was filed for BIOT: the failure is not a late amendment
   but a **failure to file an initial Schedule 13D**, due within 5 business days of
   crossing 5% (as amended effective February 2024).
3. If an initial Schedule 13D was filed: Rule 13d-2(a) requires prompt amendment on any
   material change, and a disposition of 1% or more is deemed material. The minimum
   established disposition here is 5.84%. Amendments are due within 2 business days.

### B-2. Rule 144(i) — former shell company. **Treat as the priority item.**
BIOT is the successor to a SPAC, i.e. a former shell company. Rule 144 is unavailable
for securities of a former shell company until at least **12 months after** Form 10 —
equivalent information has been filed, and while the issuer remains current in its
reporting.

If that analysis holds, Rule 144 was **not available to any holder in July or August
2026**, and would not become available until approximately July 2027.

Accordingly, please determine:

1. Is there an **effective resale registration statement** covering the sponsor shares?
2. If not, under what exemption were these shares sold into the market?
3. If no exemption was available, does this constitute an **unregistered distribution
   under Section 5 of the Securities Act**, giving purchasers a rescission right under
   Section 12(a)(1)?
4. Were any **Form 144** filings made? Affiliates selling more than 5,000 shares or
   $50,000 in any three-month period must file.
5. If the sponsor entities are affiliates, the Rule 144(e) volume limitation is the
   greater of 1% of outstanding (288,097 shares) or average weekly trading volume, per
   three-month period. The established minimum disposition of 1,681,822 shares in
   approximately one month exceeds that limit by a wide margin.

### B-3. Legend removal — internal governance question
The removal of restrictive legends on 2,855,381 shares on the listing date required an
instruction to Continental Stock Transfer & Trust, ordinarily accompanied by an opinion
of counsel. Please obtain from the transfer agent:

1. The instruction letters and any legal opinions supporting the July 24, 2026 legend
   removals;
2. Who authorized them, and on whose behalf;
3. Whether the Company or its counsel was the source of the instruction.

### B-4. Lock-up
Please review the business combination agreement, sponsor letter agreement, and any
registration rights agreement for lock-up covenants applicable to the sponsor entities,
and advise whether the July 24, 2026 legend removals and subsequent DTC deposits are
consistent with them.

### B-5. Section 16
BIOT files on Form 20-F. Section 16 does not apply to foreign private issuers
(Rule 3a12-3(b)), so no Form 4 would exist. Section 13(d) does apply. Please confirm.
